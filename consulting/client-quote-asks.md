# Client quote asks

Two clients, two short quotes: that is enough proof for the site. This doc has the ask messages to send and the spot where the quotes will go once approved.

## What we need from each client

One or two sentences, in their words, about what changed. A number makes it stronger ("missed calls went to zero", "saved about five hours a week") but is not required. We publish first name (or full name if they prefer), business type, and city if they are comfortable. No logos, no lengthy testimonial.

## Ask message (email or text, adjust per client)

> Hi [name] — quick favor. I'm putting client words on my site instead of marketing copy. Could you give me one or two sentences on what the [system/work] has actually done for you day to day? A rough number helps if one comes to mind (time saved, calls caught, whatever is true). I'd list it as "[first name], [business type]" — or your full name and business name if you'd rather have the link. Totally fine to say no.

Notes:
- Ask for what is true, not what sounds good. A modest real quote beats an inflated one.
- Confirm in writing how they want to be credited before publishing.
- If they offer to "let you write it and they'll approve," draft from something they actually said and get an explicit OK.

## Where the quotes go on the site

Planned placement: a short "client notes" row in the packages section (or under the Proof section) on the homepage, styled like the rest of the working wall. One line per client:

> "[quote]" — [first name], [business type]

Implementation is a small copy-in once both quotes and credit lines are approved. Any Claude session can add it; point it at this doc and the approved text.
