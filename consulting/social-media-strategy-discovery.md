## Part 2: Questions for you

Answer what you can. The starred (★) ones matter most. Skips are fine, I'll flag assumptions.

### A. Goal and motivation (why do this at all)
1. ★ What's the real outcome you want in 12 months? Rank these or add your own: inbound leads/customers for Vora, audience/influence for its own sake, career optionality (new roles, raises, recruiting pull), fundraising credibility, selling info products or a paid community, speaking/consulting income, hiring talent.
2. ★ If you could only have one win 90 days from now, what would it be?
3. How will you know this is working? Followers, qualified inbound DMs, newsletter signups, Vora signups, revenue, job offers? Pick the metric you'd actually check.
4. Is there a deadline or event driving this (a Vora launch, a fundraise, a job search)?

### B. Positioning and the crypto-to-AI question
5. ★ Pick the relationship between your AI identity and your blockchain background:
   (a) Full pivot. Bury crypto, go all-in on AI.
   (b) Bridge. "AI x institutional/enterprise infrastructure" as the unique angle.
   (c) Parallel tracks. AI as the new lead, crypto kept as credibility.
6. ★ Does Blockdaemon (your employer) constrain what you can post? Any non-compete, social policy, or sensitivity about you publicly building a separate AI company (Vora) and brand?
7. ★ In one sentence, who is the audience you want? Examples: technical founders, indie hackers/solopreneurs, enterprise/AI decision-makers, other AI engineers, small-business owners wanting automation. Be specific. "Everyone" kills the strategy.
8. What do you want to be *known for* in five words or less? (e.g., "ships AI agents in public," "AI for real businesses," "enterprise AI infrastructure").
9. Is Vora the centerpiece of the brand, or a thing you happen to be building behind a personal brand? (Personal brand first vs product first changes everything.)

### C. Proof and "building in public"
10. ★ How comfortable are you open-sourcing or showing your private work? Options: open-source select repos, post code snippets/screenshots only, write about what you built without showing code, keep it all private.
11. ★ Of your private projects (Vora, ai-life-agent, dev-brain, control-center, trading-agents, the assistants), which are you proud of AND allowed to talk about publicly?
12. Anything that must stay confidential (client work, Blockdaemon-related, Vora stealth features)?
13. Is Vora public/launched, in stealth, or pre-launch? Can it be named publicly today?

### D. Voice and persona
14. ★ Which voice fits you? Builder-in-public (raw, honest, shows the work), Teacher/explainer (makes hard AI things simple), Contrarian/opinion (strong takes, debate), Curator/analyst (synthesizes the firehose, like your AI journal), Founder-narrator (story of building Vora). Pick one or two.
15. How personal are you willing to get? Just the work, or also the journey, failures, money, life?
16. Whose presence do you admire and want yours to feel like? Name 2-3 accounts (AI or otherwise). Tells me tone and format fast.
17. Any topics, takes, or aesthetics you want to avoid being associated with (including crypto-bro signaling)?

### E. Content and format
18. ★ What do you actually enjoy making? Be honest, you'll only sustain what you like: short text posts/threads, long-form essays, code/demos/screen recordings, talking-head video, carousels/visuals, voice notes/podcast.
19. You already run a daily AI intelligence journal. Should that become public-facing content (newsletter, threads) instead of just a private Discord feed?
20. What are 3-5 topics you could talk about for an hour with no prep? These become your content pillars.
21. Do you want to write everything yourself, or have me/AI draft from your raw inputs (your commits, notes, the daily journal) for you to edit?

### F. Platforms and cadence
22. ★ Where should the energy go? Rank: X, LinkedIn, GitHub, YouTube, a newsletter, the Discord community, TikTok/IG, Threads/Bluesky. (My instinct: X + LinkedIn primary, GitHub + newsletter as proof/depth, but tell me your gut.)
23. ★ Realistic time budget per week for content, total? (e.g., 2 hrs, 5 hrs, 10 hrs.) This sets the whole cadence.
24. What's the role of Facebook? Keep personal/private, repurpose, or ignore for this?
25. Do you want a unified handle? `cbolden15_` on X is off-pattern from `cbolden15` everywhere else. Worth trying to fix, or leave it?

### G. Assets, resources, budget
26. Is calebbolden.com live, and does the blog have real posts yet? Should the site be the hub everything points to?
27. What's the status of the ai-tech-collective Discord. Real members, or just you and the bot right now?
28. Any existing email list, newsletter, or audience anywhere I should know about?
29. Budget for this (tools, design, scheduling, possible editor/VA, ads)? Rough monthly number or "near zero."
30. Do you want to be on camera? (Yes/no/maybe changes whether YouTube/short-form is on the table.)

### H. Constraints and risk
31. Anything off-limits to post about (employer, clients, family, location, income)?
32. Any past content you'd want scrubbed or distanced from (old crypto takes, dormant accounts)?
33. How fast do you want to move? Aggressive 30-day relaunch vs steady 90-day ramp?
