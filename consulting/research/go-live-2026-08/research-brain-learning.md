## Marketing

- **Sell the transformation/outcome, not "AI."** Buyers don't buy the technology; they buy the result. Niche narrowly rather than staying a generalist "AI agency." — *ai-agencies-websites.md*
- **VSL (video sales letter) before every sales call.** One operator credited a 2-5 minute video sales letter, watched before any call, as the single highest-leverage change that scaled a business from $100k/mo to $470k+/mo. — *ai-agencies-websites.md*
- **60/30/10 content formula** and the **"1-1-1 rule"** for content mix (recurring framework across multiple sources, exact ratios logged as: 60% authority-building, 30% engagement, 10% direct offer — treat as directional). — *ai-agencies-websites.md*
- **Eugene Schwartz awareness continuum** — match marketing message to where the prospect sits (unaware → problem-aware → solution-aware → product-aware → most-aware) rather than pitching product-aware copy to an unaware audience. — *offers-pricing.md*
- **Local SEO: one page per service category, mirroring your Google Business Profile structure, all interlinked.** Title tag/H1 = "primary category + city." Avoid thin city-gating doorway pages; for multi-location businesses build a full page set per location. Content depth is the single biggest ranking lever. — *seo.md*
- **NAP consistency** (name/address/phone matched everywhere) and concentrated internal link authority plus above-fold trust signals. — *seo.md*
- **Weak-competitor signals to target in local SEO/outreach:** fewer than 10 reviews, no real website, no exact-match domain. — *seo.md*
- **Backlink strategy:** dofollow, one link per domain, filter spammy sources; editorial links beat directory links; cross-post to high-DR platforms. — *seo.md*
- **Zero-click marketing reality:** 58.5% of searches end with no click; roughly 360 of every 1,000 people reached are on the open web. Post standalone-value content (not promotional) at roughly a 5:1 ratio to promotional content, 1-2 zero-click posts/week on 2-3 channels, and route that "rented land" traffic to an owned email list. — *seo.md*
- **Content-as-service:** a 10-field intake form can package "we'll write your SEO/zero-click content" as a standalone offer. — *seo.md*
- **Premium-website psychology:** ~50ms for a visitor to form an opinion of a website; cognitive fluency (simple, clear, intuitive = perceived as more trustworthy); peak-end rule (people remember the peaks and the ending, not the average); the effect is "based on respect," not manipulation tricks. — *general.md*
- **Screenshot and publicly share client results/testimonials early**, especially with few clients, to generate referrals. — *general.md* ("$135K-Month AI Website Business")
- **Reciprocity in outreach:** give something genuinely and unconditionally before asking for a favor — strings-attached giving kills the reciprocity effect. Cite similar peers (not celebrities) who already agreed to what's being proposed. Highlight genuine limits/loss rather than fabricated scarcity. — *general.md*
- **Ask Maps competitive-gap outreach:** scan Google Maps for local businesses with strong ratings but few reviews vs. their competitors, then reach out with a 2-3 minute screen-share video pointing at the specific gap. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **Permission-ask before sending an unsolicited audit.** Ask first, then send a free 2-minute Loom audit video; follow up at most twice. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **Free/paid AI-visibility audit** (how a business shows up across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude) as a sales wedge. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **Ad copy at 5th-grade reading level**, checked with the Hemingway Editor; keep Meta ad targeting broad (location + age only) rather than over-narrowing. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **DM creators with 25k+ views** using the literal first line "paid promo for [app]" to source influencer/affiliate promotion cheaply. — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **Hold paid ads until you have a real budget** (one source specifically says $3,000) and rely on influencer/organic outreach until then; test organic resonance before scaling into paid. — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **~200 LinkedIn connection requests/week, up to 3 follow-ups**, as a volume outbound-content channel. — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **Personalize cold outreach with one specific detail** (a funding raise, low LinkedIn engagement, etc.) rather than generic personalization tokens. — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **Outbound sequence framework** (recurring, detailed in *general.md*): pick 2 problems max per buyer persona; find ONE strong research trigger and reuse it across every channel (email, LinkedIn, voicemail, cold call) instead of researching a new angle per channel; structure outreach in 3 phases (biggest problem → second problem → breakup), phasing out LinkedIn then calls as the sequence progresses; give each new problem/phase its own short non-salesy subject line; cap total sequences at 3-5 (one per persona/product) with swappable first-touch trigger templates; after every ~100 prospects, review reply rate by segment/template and change exactly one underperforming template before retesting; rank CTAs offer > interest > time-based. — *general.md*, "The Perfect Outbound Sequence"

## Sales

- **CLOSING call framework:** Comfort, Learn, Obstacles, Stakes, Introduce, Narrow, Get paid. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **Open sales calls with a diagnostic question**, not a pitch. On local-business calls specifically: ask "what's the biggest thing blocking your customer growth" and listen fully before pitching. — *offers-pricing.md*, *local-business-ai.md*
- **1-10 readiness rating** ("on a scale of 1-10, how ready are you to move forward") followed by "what's stopping a 10" as the objection-surfacing follow-up. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **Direct-price close script** for the common "how does it work" question — state the price directly rather than deflecting into more discovery. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **Track close rate as a pricing signal:** above 50% close rate means you're underpriced; target 20-30%. — *offers-pricing.md*, echoed in *ai-agencies-websites.md*
- **Cap sales calls to 1-2 fixed days per week** rather than taking them ad hoc. — *offers-pricing.md*
- **Objections mean "not yet believing they need it badly enough."** Log every objection and redesign the specific offer element causing it, rather than scripting harder rebuttals. — *offers-pricing.md*
- **Cold-call filters** for local-business prospecting: 5+ reviews and a verified Google Business Profile listing, to screen out businesses too early-stage to buy. — *ai-agencies-websites.md*
- **Prioritize cold calling over cold email or purchased lead lists** for local-business outreach. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **Sales negotiation tactics** (Cialdini-adjacent, from "49 Psychological Mind Tricks"): open with a number first (high when selling, low when buying) to anchor; offer two options you'd be equally happy with instead of an open-ended choice; approach people early in the day for decisions needing careful thought, later in the day for easier compliance; acknowledge objections openly and pre-address likely concerns; prepare both sides' interests and alternatives before negotiating, and negotiate on interests, not stated positions; sanity-check any influence technique by asking if you'd be comfortable if it were fully transparent to the other person; prefer video over phone, phone over email, when the message depends on non-verbal cues. *(Video's own open-questions section flags some cited priming studies as failed-to-replicate or effect-size-inflated — treat the psychology framing skeptically, the tactics themselves are lower-risk.)* — *general.md*
- **Gong 300M-call study cited:** cold call gets roughly 2x reply rate of email follow-up; voicemail gets roughly 3x; diminishing returns after 2 voicemails to the same prospect. — *general.md*
- **Record sales pitches as a Loom with the prospect's LinkedIn visible**, and reflect back what the prospect already tried/failed as the basis for the offer. — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **Pitch a hypothetical product on calls; commit to a 1-2 week build only after getting a "yes."** — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **On sales calls: identify the dream outcome and the blocker, frame the service as the fastest path there, and name the cost of inaction.** — *solo-side-businesses.md*

## Offer / Pricing

AI-consulting-specific offer ladder, sourced from "7 AI services boomer businesses are BEGGING to buy" (via *offers-pricing.md*) — self-reported case-study figures, not independently verified:
- $999 45-minute diagnostic assessment; source claims 50-60% convert to paid implementation. Built by feeding a Fathom call transcript into Claude for extraction.
- $1,000-2,000/mo "AI concierge" retainer.
- $3,500 fixed-price process redesign.
- $1,000-3,000 Zapier/Make automation builds.
- $3,000 custom GPT/Claude project.
- $3,000-5,000 Claude skills library + orchestrator.
- $5,000-10,000+ full implementation, plus roughly 10% of that monthly for maintenance.
- Delivery pattern: intake form → Claude skill → structured context files → client-facing Claude project.
- **Always start with a paid assessment**, not a free one.

- **Stacked/bundled pricing beats single-service pricing for churn.** Example cited: $297 + $397 + $497 = $1,191/mo per client, with upsell timing at 60-90 days and again around month 5-6. — *ai-agencies-websites.md*
- **Beginner agency pricing should stay under roughly $800/mo** until you have proof and case studies. — *ai-agencies-websites.md*
- **Standard local-AI-agency deal structure:** ~$2,500 onboarding fee + ~$1,000/mo retainer. Service-menu examples: video-clip service $1,500-2,500/mo, email-writing service $2,000-3,000/mo. — *ai-agencies-websites.md*
- **"Fractional AI officer" positioning:** audit and consolidate a client's scattered tech stack into one CRM, rather than selling point-solution tools. — *ai-agencies-websites.md*
- **Value ladder example** (repeatable pattern, not literal template): free/cheap entry (bootcamp) → $199-299 product → $8-10k custom build → ongoing maintenance → $X retreat/mastermind → capped high-touch "growth partner" tier at the top. — *ai-agencies-websites.md*
- **Hold your highest-value backend service back as a bonus-then-sell**, rather than leading with it — backend revenue is claimed to run 3-5x front-end revenue once trust is established. — *offers-pricing.md*
- **Replace discounts with flexible payment terms.** Attach any guarantee to a specific stated outcome, not a vague "satisfaction" promise. Use a short money-back window paired with a refundable deposit rather than a long open-ended guarantee. — *offers-pricing.md*
- **Position against a category** ("anti-agency," etc.) rather than only against named competitors. — *offers-pricing.md*
- **Quote services as a recurring monthly rate, not a lump sum**, and track prep/follow-up/delivery time per client to catch scope creep before it erodes margin. — *offers-pricing.md*
- **Adding a 1:1 tier on top of an existing course/product broke a revenue plateau** for one case study (Leila Pomper — self-reported, tripled revenue after a $600-700k/yr ceiling). Pattern: when a scalable low-touch offer plateaus, a high-touch tier above it can unlock the next tier of revenue. — *offers-pricing.md*
- **Local-business flat-rate offer:** $500/mo flat, no contract, with a 60-day money-back guarantee tied to a measurable outcome (review count or call volume improvement). — *local-business-ai.md*
- **Bundle pricing tied to payments/labor actually completed**, rather than flat regardless of usage. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **Van Westendorp pricing method**, cited via Hormozi content (flagged in the video's own open-questions as needing independent verification of attribution): ask customers four questions — too expensive to consider, too cheap to be credible, borderline but would buy, and a bargain — then plot responses to find the optimal price band. — *general.md*
- **Price to willingness-to-pay based on the outcome delivered, not to your perceived effort.** Niching becomes necessary once a business crosses roughly $1-3M revenue (Hormozi framing). — *general.md*
- **"I help X achieve Y by doing Z" one-sentence offer test** — if you can't state the offer in one sentence, it isn't a real offer yet. Same framework appears as "the offer triangle" (what / who / price) across multiple sources. — *solo-side-businesses.md*, *offers-pricing.md*, *general.md*
- **Test a $47 paid entry tier** to filter for commitment when a free community is converting weakly. — *solo-side-businesses.md*

## Operations

- **Only two real growth levers exist: new-customer acquisition and upselling existing clients.** Most operators spend only 1-2 hours/day on actual revenue-producing activities (RPA framing, Hormozi-adjacent) — do 3-5 revenue-producing activities every day before touching admin work. — *ai-agencies-websites.md*, *general.md*
- **Client selection is "literally 90% of churn."** Screening the wrong-fit clients out matters more than almost anything else for agency retention. — *ai-agencies-websites.md*
- **Claude workflow tactics for agency delivery:** screenshot a competitor's site and use it as the creative brief; draft-not-send email replies inside Gmail for human review before send; package a repeated workflow as a reusable Claude Skill; run an SEO/performance pass as a standard deliverable step. — *ai-agencies-websites.md*
- **Embed in the target business and do the manual work by hand first**, before building the automation that replaces it — validates the workflow and produces the training data/proof-of-concept simultaneously. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **Validate a pain point only if 3 of 5 interviewed businesses independently name the same problem.** Pick one niche and one city exclusively before expanding. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **8 sellable Claude Skills for local businesses**, each screen-recorded from doing the task manually first: review-response drafting (trained on 6-7 past replies), database reactivation (reframe as a % — e.g., 2% of 1,100 leads = 22 recovered customers), quote generation from a voice memo (cuts 2-day turnaround to minutes), intake-triage + onboarding-packet bundle, local-search/GEO content, proposal generation from discovery notes, government-bid monitoring + drafting. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **10-15 warm contacts before any cold outreach.** Target markets with no software incumbent at all (i.e., the prior "incumbent" was a human doing it manually, not a competing tool). — *local-business-ai.md*
- **AI receptionist picks up after 10 seconds of no answer**; 4-message automated review-request sequence triggered by job completion; text-based (not email) lead reactivation, up to 6 follow-ups; same review-request link used for every customer for policy compliance; one-question-at-a-time incentivized mobile intake forms. — *local-business-ai.md*
- **6-step follow-up/review/sales-training system should be running before turning on paid ads.** — *local-business-ai.md*
- **White-label AI-employee onboarding**: provision a website/chat/voice/booking/inbox stack directly from a client's Google Business Profile link, keeping 100% of client payments (vs. rev-share). — *local-business-ai.md*
- **Track ~100 businesses per niche/city in a spreadsheet** (name, site, prices, reviews, complaints, hiring signals) as a standing research asset — the data itself becomes sellable to agencies/consultants serving that niche. — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **Build 1-3 focused Claude Skills rather than a full automation platform**, and pair an existing human service with Claude to automate roughly 90% of it rather than 100%. — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **Escalate delivery format over time for retention** (PDF → live calls → live dashboards), and replace 1:1 sales calls with a weekly workshop/Q&A once volume makes 1:1 uneconomical — calculate total monthly 1:1 hours (members × call length) before assuming a model can't scale. — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **Track weekly outbound reply rate; if it's under 1% on 500 sends, revise the offer or the customer profile**, not just the copy. — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **8+ paragraph customer profile per segment** covering mindset and existing tools, not just demographics. — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **Automated agent-built financial back office (self-reported case study):** on contract signature, an agent extracts terms and auto-drafts (not auto-sends) the QuickBooks invoice for human review; an agent auto-follows-up on late payment per contract terms, escalating to a human if unpaid past 2 weeks rather than continuing to auto-follow-up indefinitely; one weekly summary email covers cash flow, expenses, income, and unpaid clients. — *ai-agents.md*, "I Built an AI Financial Operating System for Business"
- **Trust in an automated pipeline should escalate gradually** — start with agents opening PRs/drafts for human review, only allow auto-merge/auto-send once confidence is established. — *ai-agents.md*
- **GTM-engineering framing** (from "I asked the world's best GTM engineer how he uses Claude Code," via *ai-agents.md*): knowledge compounds only within a knowable, niche customer universe — mixing verticals fractures outbound effectiveness; target whoever has the largest quantifiable consequence from the problem, not just whoever might buy; do manual research by hand first, then have Claude abstract the observed pattern into a reusable agent; the best research harnesses try free/cheap data sources first and escalate to paid calls only when necessary.
- **PPF Plan** (Personal / Professional / Financial) — set 1/3/5/10-year targets across all three for yourself and any team, framed as "equity" in a small business. — *general.md*
- **5 causes of noncompliance** management framework — when a direct report or process underperforms, diagnose which of five causes applies rather than assuming or attacking character (framework named but the five causes themselves weren't enumerated in the source excerpt). — *general.md*
- **5-stage business lifecycle:** start, grow, optimize, scale, exit — identify which stage each part of the business is actually in before deciding what to build next. — *general.md*

- **3-mechanic directory-monetization model:** free listing → paid featured placement, banner ads, lead auction via email — cited as a generalizable small-revenue-stream stack, not AI-consulting-specific. — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **Hire a VA at your first $1,000 in revenue** — a specific, early trigger point rather than "once you can afford it." — *solo-side-businesses.md*
- **Calculate your own expected lifetime earnings before big career/business decisions** rather than relying on gut feel. — *solo-side-businesses.md*

## Other

- **Zero Token Architecture (Kelsey Hightower, PlatformCon 2026):** infer once with an agent to build a repeatable tool (e.g., a DB-table generator or CI/CD step), then export and reuse it without further inference — relevant to Caleb's own Claude-Skills-as-deliverable service pattern above. Companion caution: don't outsource your own fundamental understanding of a system to the agent; periodically ask how you're better than you were two years ago as a check against over-reliance. — *general.md*
- **SaaS-buyer defensibility test (2026 M&A framing, one-operator anecdote):** the real test of a defensible SaaS product in the AI era is "would a competitor at half the price actually be able to win my customers away" — relevant framing for productizing a consulting offer into something with real moat rather than a commodity retainer. Five cited moats: hardware lock-in, two-sided marketplace, system-of-record status, proprietary data that doesn't leak back out via API, and genuine switching cost. — *general.md*
- **Reading list** referenced across multiple sources as background for operators building a service business: *The Millionaire Fastlane*, *Unscripted*, *They Made America*, *Orange County Inc.*, *The Art of the Start*, *The Right Exit Strategy*, *How to Get Rich* (Felix Dennis), *Buy Back Your Time*, *The Lean Startup*, *The Innovator's Dilemma*, *Crossing the Chasm*, *How to Be a Rainmaker*, *Influence* (Cialdini), *Traction*, *Never Split the Difference*, *Never Eat Alone*, *Multipliers*, *The Effective Executive*, *Shoe Dog*, *The Hard Thing About Hard Things*, *The Inner Game of Tennis*. — *solo-side-businesses.md*, *general.md*
