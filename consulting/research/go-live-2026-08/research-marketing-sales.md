# Marketing + sales launch artifacts for a solo AI consultant (local SMB, Fort Worth/DFW)

Context: Vora Technologies LLC, calebbolden.com already live with service packages, two interactive
lead-gen tools, gated PDF magnets, chat capture, and a blog. First-30-days channels: cold email,
referrals, social. Budget $2,000 pre-first-client. Capacity 25 hrs/week. No analytics, no email list
provider, no Calendly link yet. Runway to launch: 11 days.

Industry grounding: positioning-first sequencing and niche-narrowing (David C. Baker / Blair Enns,
Win Without Pitching), value-based proposal + discovery-call method (Jonathan Stark's 5-page proposal
and "Why Conversation"), anchor-client / client-zero patterns for building proof with no clients yet,
plus 2026 cold-email deliverability and local-SEO benchmarks. Sources inline per section.

## MARKETING

### Required to launch (do before going public)

| Artifact | Done looks like | Effort | Dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positioning statement / niche definition | One sentence: who you serve (local DFW small businesses, which verticals), what problem you solve, why you're credible. Baker/Enns both treat this as the foundational item — "nothing else matters until you get this part right," since a vague positioning forces competing on price. Already partially done via the two lead-gen tools' framing; write it down as a standalone artifact and use it verbatim on the homepage, LinkedIn headline, and cold-email signature. | 2-3 hrs | None — do first, everything else references it |
| Google Business Profile | Claimed, verified (video verification is common in 2026), category set correctly, service-area business config (up to 20 areas) if no storefront, 750-char description with service + area keywords, hours/phone/website filled. 46% of Google searches have local intent and 76% of "near me" searchers visit within 24 hrs, so this is a hard launch-blocking item for a *local* practice. | 1-2 hrs setup + up to weeks for verification mail | Verification can take days — start this first, in parallel with everything else |
| Proof of work without clients — pilot/anchor offer | A named, bounded pilot offer (e.g., a paid AI-readiness audit or 2-week diagnostic) that becomes your first 1-2 case studies. "Client zero" pattern: run the two live tools (readiness scorecard, revenue-leak calculator) on your own business or a friendly local business first, publish that as a proof artifact even before a paying client exists. Anchor clients later should be capped at 15-35% of revenue each. | 3-5 hrs to package the pilot offer + write-up | None |
| Case study / proof template (even if empty) | A reusable structure (client context → pain points → what you did → quantified + qualitative result) ready to fill the moment the pilot completes. Consultport's structure is standard industry practice. | 1 hr | Needs the pilot to actually run before it has content |
| LinkedIn profile optimized as primary channel | Personal profile (not company page — LinkedIn's algorithm favors personal posts, and 4/5 members are decision-makers), headline = positioning statement, About section states the niche, banner/CTA point to calebbolden.com tools. LinkedIn converts at 2.74% vs Facebook's 0.77% for B2B and is the standard first channel for a consulting launch. | 2 hrs | Needs positioning statement finished first |
| Launch announcement (LinkedIn post + email to personal network) | One LinkedIn post + one email to your existing contacts announcing the practice, the niche, and a soft CTA (book a free scorecard/calculator run). Standard solo-launch pattern combines the announcement with a referral ask. | 2 hrs | Needs GBP + LinkedIn ready so links land somewhere real |
| Minimal analytics | At minimum, confirm the two tools' `[lead]` / `[lead-magnet]` log lines are being captured (already true per this project's CLAUDE.md) — real dashboard analytics (Umami/Plausible) is already flagged in this repo's CLAUDE.md as a pre-promotion blocker and is correctly NOT required for the 11-day launch, just needed before serious ad spend or scale. | 0 hrs now (already logged) | — |

### Nice-to-have / first-90-days (not launch-blocking)

| Artifact | Done looks like | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook Business Page | Secondary channel; useful for local community visibility and cheaper retargeting once LinkedIn generates awareness, per the 2026 hybrid-channel consensus. Not required day one for a B2B-only local practice. | 1 hr |
| Content cadence / thought-leadership calendar | Baker's own launch playbook was writing ~55 highly specific, pain-point-driven pieces; that's a 90-day project, not an 11-day one. Start with 1 post/week max. | ongoing, 1-2 hrs/week |
| Self-hosted analytics (Umami/Plausible) | Already on this project's "next up" list — do before any paid promotion, not before launch. | 2-4 hrs |
| Local citations / directory consistency (NAP) | Beyond GBP: Yelp, Bing Places, Chamber of Commerce listing, industry directories. Improves local pack ranking over 60-90 days but doesn't block a cold-email/referral launch. | 2-3 hrs |
| Email list provider + nurture sequence | Already flagged in this repo's CLAUDE.md as "no list provider yet; at current volume send manually." Correct call — defer until volume justifies it. | deferred |
| Formal case studies (multiple, polished) | One pilot write-up is enough to launch; a library of 3-5 case studies with anonymized data is a 90-day goal per Consultport's standard structure. | 2-3 hrs each |

### Commonly overdone at launch (per this research)

1. **Full content marketing engine before any positioning validation.** Baker explicitly warns that fear of insufficient work causes specialists to dilute into generalists — publishing broadly before the niche is locked wastes the 90-day window.
2. **Multi-platform social presence.** Data is unambiguous that LinkedIn alone outperforms Facebook 3.5x on B2B lead conversion; spreading thin across Instagram/Facebook/X before LinkedIn is dialed in is wasted effort for THIS audience (B2B local decision-makers).
3. **Perfect, agency-style case studies.** Consultants who wait for a "real enough" client before publishing proof delay launch unnecessarily — the client-zero / pilot-offer pattern exists specifically to avoid this trap.

## SALES

### Required to launch

| Artifact | Done looks like | Effort | Dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery call script/agenda | Jonathan Stark's "Why Conversation" framework: a structured set of "why" questions that surface the prospect's real problem and build the fee justification, rather than accepting their self-diagnosed solution. Adapt to a 20-30 min local-SMB call. Not a rigid script — a question set + agenda. | 2 hrs | Needs positioning done so you know who's qualified |
| Proposal template | Jonathan Stark's 5-page structure: outcomes-first framing, 2-3 priced options, price stated only after value is established, fixed quote (not hourly estimate). His stated result: 80%+ close rate using this exact structure. Free template exists at jonathanstark.com/5-page-proposal-template — adapt, don't rebuild from scratch. | 2-3 hrs to adapt + brand | Needs pricing decided (see below) |
| Pricing structure decision | Value-based, not hourly — Stark's core thesis (hourly billing caps upside and misaligns incentives). Set 2-3 packaged tiers tied to outcomes (already partially done per this project's "service packages" on the site — confirm they map to this proposal structure). | 2 hrs | Feeds proposal template |
| Lead qualification criteria | A short written checklist (business size, budget signal, urgency, decision-maker access) so the two lead-gen tools and cold email don't burn discovery-call time on unqualified leads. | 1 hr | None |
| Lightweight CRM / pipeline tracker | For a true solo operator at launch volume, Streak (Gmail-native, free tier) or a single Notion/Airtable board is sufficient — HubSpot Free is more complete but flagged in multiple 2026 reviews as "overkill/cluttered" for a true solo. Recommendation: start with a simple Notion or Airtable pipeline board (stages: cold → replied → discovery call → proposal sent → won/lost); upgrade only past ~5 concurrent active leads. | 1-2 hrs | None |
| Cold email sequence (structure + infrastructure) | 3-4 email sequence, personalized per local business, sent from a **secondary domain** (never the primary calebbolden.com domain) with SPF/DKIM/DMARC configured. **Hard dependency: domain warming.** New domain needs a 2-4 week warmup ramp (Week 1: 5-10/day warmup only, Week 2: 15-20/day, Week 3+: add 5-10 real cold/day) before real volume — this alone may not fit inside 11 days if not already started. Cap at ~25-30 sends/day per mailbox once warm. Google/Yahoo/Microsoft enforce <0.3% spam complaints, <2% bounce as of 2026. | 3-4 hrs setup + **2-4 weeks unavoidable warmup lead time** | **BLOCKING for a domain not yet warming — start immediately regardless of other launch tasks** |
| Referral-ask template | One reusable email, sent to existing personal/professional network at launch, pairing the "new practice" announcement with a direct, specific referral ask ("if you know a local business owner who...", not a generic ask). Best practice: don't ask for a referral in the very first outreach message to a stranger — this is for your warm network only at launch. | 1 hr | Pairs with the launch announcement email above |
| Objection-handling notes for AI-skeptical SMB owners | A short written reference (not a rigid script) covering the recurring objections a local small-business owner will raise about AI specifically (job-loss fear, "we tried a chatbot and it failed," data privacy, "too complicated for us," cost skepticism). 2026 objection-handling consensus: diagnose the real concern before responding (Acknowledge → Diagnose → Reframe → Bridge), don't argue past it — skeptical buyers detect canned rebuttals immediately. | 2 hrs | None |

### Nice-to-have / first-90-days

| Artifact | Done looks like | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Formal follow-up nurture sequences (post-discovery-call, post-proposal) | Automated multi-touch follow-up beyond a manual reminder. At launch volume, manual follow-up in the CRM is sufficient. | 2-3 hrs |
| Structured referral *program* (incentives, tracking) | Formal referral program mechanics (credits, % finder's fee) matter once repeat referral volume exists; at zero clients, the ask-template above is sufficient. | deferred |
| Calendly booking link | Already on this repo's "Caleb-side" unblock list — needed for smooth discovery-call scheduling but not a hard launch blocker (can coordinate by email for the first few calls). | 15 min once created |
| Proposal Teardown / external review of your proposal | Stark offers a paid review service; useful once you have a real proposal to test, not before. | deferred |
| CRM upgrade to HubSpot Free or paid tool | Correct only once lead volume exceeds ~5 concurrent active prospects, per multiple 2026 solo-CRM comparisons. | deferred |

### Commonly overdone at launch

1. **Building a full CRM/sales-ops stack before any leads exist.** Multiple 2026 sources agree: a spreadsheet or single Notion/Airtable board is enough until you cross ~5 concurrent leads; HubSpot's free tier is explicitly called "overkill" for a true solo at this stage.
2. **Treating objection-handling as a memorized script.** The 2026 consensus across all objection-handling sources is that a scripted rebuttal is detectable and destroys trust with a skeptical buyer — the artifact needed is a diagnostic question set, not a word-for-word script.
3. **Sending cold email before the domain is warmed.** This is the single most common shortcut that tanks deliverability — starting high-volume sends on a brand-new domain lands in spam from day one and can burn the domain's reputation before the campaign starts.

## Top 5 launch-blocking artifacts (do these first, in this order)

1. **Positioning statement** — everything else (GBP description, LinkedIn headline, proposal, cold email) quotes this. 2-3 hrs.
2. **Cold-email sending domain + warmup START** — the only item with a multi-week fixed lead time; if not already warming, start today regardless of what else is in progress. 3-4 hrs setup, 2-4 weeks unavoidable wait.
3. **Google Business Profile claim + verification** — verification mail/video can take days; start in parallel with #1.
4. **Pilot/anchor offer + proof-of-work write-up** — your only credibility asset with zero clients; run it on yourself or a friendly local business now.
5. **Proposal template + discovery-call question set (Stark's 5-page + Why Conversation)** — needed the moment the first real conversation happens; adapt existing free templates rather than building from scratch.

## Top 3 things commonly overdone at launch

1. Building sales infrastructure (CRM, referral program, nurture automation) sized for a business that doesn't exist yet — a single pipeline board is enough until ~5 active leads.
2. Multi-platform social presence — LinkedIn alone outperforms Facebook 3.5x for this exact audience (local B2B decision-makers); spreading thin dilutes the 90-day window Baker/Enns say positioning needs to compound.
3. Waiting for a "real" paying client before publishing any proof — the client-zero/pilot-offer pattern exists specifically so a solo consultant can launch with credible proof before the first invoice.
