# The revenue leak worksheet: 5 numbers most owners never calculate

*Fifteen minutes with a calculator. Five places local businesses lose money quietly. Fill in your own numbers and see what the leaks cost you per month.*

---

None of these leaks show up in your books, because money you never collected never gets recorded. That is why they survive for years. Each section below gives you the evidence that the leak is real, then the math to size your own.

Grab last month's numbers where you have them. Estimate honestly where you don't; you can tighten the numbers later.

## Leak 1: the calls nobody answers

An observational study of 85 small businesses across 58 industries found only 38% of inbound calls get answered live. The rest hit voicemail or ring out, and most callers who reach voicemail simply hang up and call the next business on the list (PCN Missed Call Revenue Study, 2026).

- Calls you miss per week (check your phone log): ______
- Portion that were probably new business (try half): ______
- Your average job or sale value: $______
- Portion of quoted jobs you normally win (e.g. 0.4): ______

**Monthly leak: missed new-business calls × 4.3 × average value × win rate = $______**

## Leak 2: the slow quote

Research on lead response going back to the original MIT lead-response study has a consistent shape: reply in minutes and you reach almost everyone; reply tomorrow and much of the interest is gone. Industry benchmarks for trades put the first detailed quote as the winner in well over half of jobs, largely regardless of price.

- Quotes you send per month: ______
- Your typical turnaround (hours or days): ______
- Quotes that go quiet with no answer: ______
- Average value of those silent quotes: $______

**Monthly leak (conservative): silent quotes × average value × 0.25 = $______**

If your turnaround is measured in days, assume a bigger share of the silence is speed, not price.

## Leak 3: the no-shows

A five-year study covering 1.6 million appointments across 64 practices found automated reminders cut no-shows by 23%. A peer-reviewed study of 1,193 appointments found SMS reminders brought no-shows down to 1.9%, the lowest of any channel tested (Sesame Communications; PMC-indexed orthodontic study).

- No-shows per month: ______
- Average value of a kept appointment: $______
- Can the slot be refilled on short notice? If not, count the full value.

**Monthly leak: no-shows × average value × 0.25 (the share reminders would recover) = $______**

## Leak 4: the reviews you never answer

98% of consumers say the quality of a business's response to reviews influences whether they use that business, and 89% expect a response at all (BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey, 2024 and 2025). Review signals are also one of the largest local ranking factors Google uses (Whitespark Local Search Ranking Factors, 2026). Unanswered reviews cost you twice: the reader who chose someone else, and the ranking you did not earn.

This one is hard to price per month, so score it instead:

- Google reviews in the last 90 days: ______
- Portion you responded to: ______ %
- New reviews per month vs your top local competitor: ______ vs ______

If your response rate is under 80% or your competitor collects reviews faster, mark this leak **open**. Fixing it costs about 30 minutes a week.

## Leak 5: the software you pay for and don't use

Zylo's 2025 SaaS Management Index found 21% of paid software licenses go completely unused, and another 45% are underutilized. For a 15-person company that is commonly thousands of dollars a year in pure waste (Ramp and related SMB software-spend analyses put it at $3,000 to $6,000).

Open your bank statement and list every monthly software charge:

- Subscriptions you pay for: ______
- Ones nobody has logged into for 60 days: ______
- Monthly total of those: $______

**Monthly leak: that total, straight off the bottom line = $______**
