# Launch week plan

Seven days of scaffolding to open the four founder accounts, ending inside the 18-week
curriculum rather than beside it. Day 7 publishes Week 1 of `consulting/social/syllabus.md`,
and Week 2 follows on the normal weekly cadence. Nothing here is a parallel calendar. After
Day 7 there is only the syllabus.

Platforms are the four from the social presence spec: LinkedIn (operators), YouTube Shorts,
Instagram Reels, Facebook Page (owners). Every day ends at the same call to action, taking
the readiness assessment at calebbolden.com/tools/ai-readiness.

## Prerequisites

Do not start Day 1 until all of these are true. They are the runbook in
`consulting/social/account-setup.md`, steps 1 through 9.

1. Handle availability checked on all four platforms before creating any of them. One handle
   across four platforms beats the preferred handle on three.
2. LinkedIn profile repositioned for operators. Vora Technologies Consulting added under
   Experience, and nowhere else.
3. YouTube Brand Account, Instagram account, and Facebook Page created under the name
   Caleb Bolden.
4. Vora org created, all four accounts connected through `social/sync-accounts`.
5. Brand-voice profile authored from scratch. Do not inherit `industry-presets.ts`, which is
   tuned for local service SMBs and will flatten the operators voice on LinkedIn.
6. Guardrails on, comment classification with the Telegram approval path on.
7. The Month 1 filming batch shot in one session. That single sitting covers the clips for
   Days 2, 3, 4, 6, and 7 of this week plus the rest of the data and systems dimension.

Time note: this week runs well above the spec's ~32 min/week steady state, mostly in
filming and account setup. It is a one-time spike, not the new baseline.

## Day 1: the announcement

**Goal.** Put a stake in the ground. State what the practice is and what makes the method
different, before any teaching content asks for attention.

**Topic.** The launch announcement, written in full at `consulting/brand/launch-announcement.md`.

**Format.** LinkedIn essay, 291 words. Trimmed ~120 word variant on the Facebook Page.

**Hook.** "Most of the AI failures I watch up close have nothing to do with the AI."

**CTA.** Take the assessment at calebbolden.com/tools/ai-readiness.

**Platforms.** LinkedIn, Facebook.

**Visual.** One photograph of a real working wall: white board, five process boxes in
blueprint blue, dotted leaders to the step that costs the most time, one amber sticky on that
step reading "automate this". No text overlay. The board is the whole image.

**Type.** Promotional (1 of 2 this week).

## Day 2: map first, then automate

**Goal.** Teach the one idea the practice is built on, in under a minute, at owner altitude.

**Topic.** Why automating an unmapped process makes the waste faster instead of removing it.

**Format.** Vertical clip, 45 to 75 seconds, talking to camera at the board.

**Hook.** "If you automate a process nobody has written down, you just made the mess faster."

**CTA.** The assessment measures whether your process is written down before it asks about
tools. Link in bio and in the description.

**Platforms.** YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels.

**Visual.** Draw the five boxes live in blueprint blue, circle the third one, slap the amber
sticky on it. The drawing is the edit; no cuts needed.

**Type.** Educational.

## Day 3: what a value stream map actually looks like

**Goal.** Show the instrument. Prove there is a method behind Day 2 rather than an opinion.

**Topic.** A walkthrough of a real map, with minutes annotated on each step and the wait time
between steps called out as the thing nobody measures.

**Format.** Vertical clip, 45 to 75 seconds, mostly board and hands. Same clip reposted to
LinkedIn with two sentences of operator framing above it.

**Hook.** "This is what I do before I recommend a single piece of software."

**CTA.** Assessment link, framed as the short version of this exercise you can run yourself.

**Platforms.** Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, LinkedIn (repost with framing).

**Visual.** Time-lapse of the board filling: blueprint-blue process boxes left to right,
dimension lines under each with real minutes, dotted leaders to the gaps between steps. One
amber sticky, placed last, on the longest gap.

**Type.** Behind the scenes.

## Day 4: the rollout that worked and still failed

**Goal.** Earn trust with a failure instead of a win. This is the day that separates the
account from vendor content.

**Topic.** A business that bought the right tool twice and switched it off twice, because the
process underneath it was never agreed on.

**Format.** Vertical clip, 45 to 75 seconds, talking head, minimal board.

**Hook.** "They bought scheduling software twice in one year, and both times it worked
exactly as advertised."

**CTA.** Assessment, framed around the process documentation questions specifically.

**Platforms.** YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels.

**Visual.** One blueprint-blue box alone on an otherwise empty white board. The camera pulls
back to show the four empty box outlines around it that were never filled in.

[placeholder: whether this is one real engagement or a composite of several. Say which in the
post. If composite, say so in the first ten seconds.]

**Type.** Storytelling.

## Day 5: the five things readiness actually measures

**Goal.** Pre-frame the assessment so Day 6 is a natural next step rather than a cold ask.

**Topic.** The five dimensions the assessment scores: data and systems, process documentation,
repetition and volume, team and buy-in, budget and focus. What each one is really testing.

**Format.** LinkedIn essay, 200 to 300 words, operator altitude (what each dimension looks like
at 40 people instead of 4). Companion vertical clip covering the same five at owner altitude.

**Hook (essay).** "Readiness has almost nothing to do with how much you know about AI. It comes
down to five things you could check this afternoon."

**Hook (clip).** "Five questions decide whether AI is worth your money right now, and none of
them are about AI."

**CTA.** Assessment, with the note that the score comes back split by dimension so you can see
which one is holding you back.

**Platforms.** LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts.

**Visual.** Five blueprint-blue boxes across the white board, each labeled in small mono caps
with one dimension name. No sticky on this one. The point is the instrument, not a
recommendation.

**Type.** Educational.

## Day 6: take the assessment

**Goal.** Convert the week's attention into scored completions, which is the only number the
scorecard tracks for these channels.

**Topic.** What the assessment gives you: a score across the five dimensions and one of three
bands. Foundations first, ready for one pilot, ready to sequence. Say plainly that most local
businesses land in the first two and that this is a normal result, not a failing grade.

**Format.** Vertical clip, 45 to 75 seconds, screen and board. Short LinkedIn post pointing at
the same thing for operators.

**Hook.** "Eighteen questions, and at the end you land in one of three places. Only one of them
means you should be buying software this quarter."

**CTA.** Explicit and direct: calebbolden.com/tools/ai-readiness, free, no call required.

**Platforms.** LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook.

**Visual.** Three blueprint-blue boxes stacked on the white board, labeled with the three band
names in mono caps. One amber sticky on "ready for one pilot".

**Type.** Promotional (2 of 2 this week).

## Day 7: hand off to Week 1 of the syllabus

**Goal.** Close the launch by starting the curriculum. From here the account has a standing
reason to post every week without another planning session.

**Topic.** Syllabus Week 1, verbatim from `consulting/social/syllabus.md`: is your customer and
job information in one place, rather than scattered across email, texts, spreadsheets, and paper?

**Format.** The standard weekly unit, both cuts. Clip: the electrician checking texts, a paper
work order on the dash, and a stale spreadsheet, then calling a repeat customer back with the
wrong quote. Essay: the 55-person electrical and HVAC contractor where a disputed service date
takes three people 20 to 30 minutes to resolve, roughly 15 times a month.

**Hook (clip).** "A customer asked him about a quote from three months ago. He checked three
places, called back, and the number was still wrong."

**Hook (essay).** "At 55 people, 'where does the truth live' stops being an annoyance and turns
into a scheduling problem."

**CTA.** Take the assessment's first section and measure this before buying anything.

**Platforms.** LinkedIn (essay), YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels (clip).

**Visual.** Three blueprint-blue boxes on the white board with no lines drawn between them,
labeled phone, paper, spreadsheet. Amber sticky underneath reading "where does the truth live".

**Type.** Educational, and the first entry in the 18-week curriculum.

## After Day 7

Week 2 of the syllabus publishes on the normal weekly cadence: one clip and one essay from one
question. No further launch content. The scorecard picks up two lines from here, assessment
completions per week tagged by band, and follower count per channel monthly. Views, likes, and
impressions stay untracked on purpose.
