# Offboarding checklist

This is a stub. It gets filled in and refined during the first real engagement, then reused as-is after that. Don't skip it just because it's early, the highest-leverage 15 minutes in the whole client lifecycle happens here, and it's easy to let it slide once a project feels "done."

## Checklist

- [ ] Final deliverables confirmed received and understood by the client (not just sent, confirmed)
- [ ] Client access revoked: any scoped accounts or vault shares closed per `client-credential-policy.md`
- [ ] Credentials rotated on anything that was shared rather than scoped (this should be rare if the credential policy was followed from kickoff)
- [ ] Runbook and working files delivered to the client, including anything in the shared folder they should keep
- [ ] Testimonial and case-study ask made, in the same conversation as the final delivery, not a separate follow-up later
- [ ] Records-retention notice sent (see below)
- [ ] Client moved to "closed" in CRM, with notes on referral potential and any upsell signal for later

## Records retention notice

Include this paragraph in the final delivery or offboarding email:

> Working files and correspondence from this engagement will be retained for 7 years from today's date, standard practice for consulting records. If you'd like your own copy of anything in the shared folder, let me know within the next 30 days. After that window, working files may be archived or deleted; final deliverables already in your possession are unaffected.

## Testimonial and case-study ask

Ask for both in the same motion, right after confirming the deliverables landed well:

"Would you be willing to give me a short testimonial about how this went? And separately, would you be open to a short case study, results only, nothing identifying beyond what we agree on together?"

Get written consent for the case study specifically, don't assume a testimonial covers it. Define editorial limits up front: what can be shared, what gets redacted, and who has final approval before publication.
