# Weekly status update template

A short written update, sent async, not a meeting. Five lines is the target, don't pad it.

---

Subject: [Client name] weekly update, [date]

Hi [name],

**Done this week:** [one or two sentences, concrete]

**Next week:** [one or two sentences, concrete]

**Blockers / need from you:** [name it directly, or "none this week"]

**Budget / timeline:** [on track, or a plain statement of what's changed and why]

Questions or concerns, just reply here.

Caleb

---

## Notes

- Send on the same day every week, pick one and stick to it.
- "Blockers / need from you" is the line most likely to get skipped when nothing's wrong. Write "none this week" rather than dropping the line, a client should never wonder if something's being hidden.
- If timeline or budget shifts, say so here first, don't let it surface for the first time on a call.
