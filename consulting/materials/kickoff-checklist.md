# Kickoff checklist

## Gate

**No kickoff until the SOW is signed AND the deposit is received.** Both, not either. If one is missing, hold the kickoff call and say why plainly: "I'll get this on the calendar the moment the deposit clears."

## Before the call

- [ ] Signed SOW confirmed in Anvil
- [ ] Deposit payment confirmed received (not just invoiced)
- [ ] Welcome email sent, including: kickoff call time, what to expect, and a request to have the right people available (owner plus whoever else will be interviewed)
- [ ] Shared client folder created, following the convention: client name → project name → date. Keep editable working files in it, not just final exports.
- [ ] Client added to CRM at the "won" stage

## The kickoff call (30 minutes, recorded)

Tell the client at the start that the call is being recorded, and why (so nothing gets lost or misremembered later in the engagement).

Agenda:

1. **Scope confirmation** (10 min). Walk through the SOW line by line. Confirm what's in scope and, just as important, what's explicitly not. Resolve any ambiguity now, not three weeks in.
2. **Communication preferences** (5 min). How often, which channel (email, text, call), and who the single point of contact is on their side.
3. **Access and credentials plan** (10 min). Explain how credential handoff works per `client-credential-policy.md`: no passwords over email or chat, a dedicated Vaultwarden collection share or a client-provisioned scoped account, MFA required. Confirm what access is actually needed for this engagement and get it moving.
4. **Next steps** (5 min). Confirm the discovery interview schedule (per `discovery-interview-guide.md`), who's being interviewed, and the date of the first weekly status update.

## After the call

- [ ] First weekly update scheduled (see `weekly-status-template.md`), first one due one week out
- [ ] Recording saved to the shared client folder
- [ ] Discovery interviews booked with the people identified in the call
