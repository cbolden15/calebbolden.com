## Master services agreement

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] ("Effective Date") between:

**Consultant:** Vora Technologies LLC, a [STATE] limited liability company, acting through Caleb Bolden, Principal ("Consultant")
Address: [ADDRESS]

**Client:** [CLIENT LEGAL NAME], a [STATE] [ENTITY TYPE] ("Client")
Address: [CLIENT ADDRESS]

Consultant and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

### 1. Definitions

1.1 **"SOW"** means a Statement of Work executed by both Parties under this Agreement that describes a specific engagement, including scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees.

1.2 **"Deliverables"** means the work product Consultant specifically creates for Client and identifies as a deliverable in an SOW.

1.3 **"AI Tools"** means third-party generative AI or machine learning services used in delivering the Services, including but not limited to large language model providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google.

1.4 **"Client Data"** means any data, documents, or information Client provides to Consultant in connection with the Services.

1.5 **"Services"** means the consulting, advisory, and implementation work described in an SOW.

### 2. Engagement model

2.1 This Agreement governs the overall relationship between the Parties. Each specific engagement is described in a separate SOW that references this Agreement and incorporates it by reference. In case of conflict between this Agreement and an SOW, the SOW controls for that engagement only.

2.2 The Parties anticipate SOWs may include, without limitation:

- **Process & AI Readiness Audit**: a fixed-scope assessment producing findings, an opportunity matrix, and a roadmap.
- **Build Sprint**: a fixed-scope implementation engagement delivering one or more automations or systems.
- **Fractional AI Operator**: a recurring monthly retainer for ongoing monitoring, iteration, and advisory work.

2.3 No SOW is binding until signed by both Parties. Verbal agreements or informal correspondence do not modify this Agreement or create an SOW.

2.4 Either Party may propose a new SOW at any time. This Agreement does not obligate either Party to enter into any particular SOW.

### 3. Fees and payment

3.1 Fees for each engagement are set out in the applicable SOW.

3.2 Unless the SOW states otherwise, Client shall pay a deposit of [DEPOSIT AMOUNT OR PERCENTAGE] upon signing the SOW, with the balance invoiced [ON DELIVERY / MONTHLY / PER MILESTONE: SPECIFY IN SOW].

3.3 Invoices are due **net 15** days from the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at [RATE, E.G. 1.5% PER MONTH OR THE MAXIMUM RATE PERMITTED BY LAW, WHICHEVER IS LOWER].

3.4 Consultant may suspend work on any SOW if an invoice under that SOW is more than [15] days past due, upon written notice to Client.

3.5 Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes. Client is responsible for any sales, use, or similar taxes other than taxes on Consultant's income.

3.6 Expenses are billable only if agreed in writing in advance and itemized in the SOW or a written amendment.

### 4. Term and termination

4.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated as provided below. Each SOW has its own term as stated in that SOW.

4.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any individual SOW for convenience upon **15 days' written notice** to the other Party.

4.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement or the SOW and fails to cure the breach within [15] days of written notice describing the breach.

4.4 Upon termination for any reason, Client shall pay Consultant for all Services performed and expenses properly incurred through the effective date of termination. Consultant shall deliver to Client all Deliverables completed as of that date, upon payment in full for the work performed.

4.5 Sections 5 (Intellectual Property), 6 (Confidentiality), 8 (Limitation of Liability), and any other provision that by its nature should survive, survive termination of this Agreement.

### 5. Intellectual property

5.1 **Deliverables.** Upon full payment of all fees due under the applicable SOW, Client owns all right, title, and interest in the Deliverables specifically created for Client under that SOW, excluding any Consultant Property (defined below) incorporated into them.

5.2 **Consultant Property.** Consultant retains all right, title, and interest in and to: (a) any methods, frameworks, templates, workshop materials, scoring models, code libraries, or tools that Consultant owned or developed before the engagement or develops independently of Client's confidential information ("Pre-Existing IP"); and (b) any general knowledge, skills, techniques, and know-how Consultant develops or refines in the course of performing the Services, to the extent it does not disclose Client's confidential information ("Residual Know-How"). Consultant grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use any Consultant Property embedded in the Deliverables solely as part of those Deliverables.

5.3 **Reuse.** Nothing in this Agreement restricts Consultant from using Residual Know-How or Pre-Existing IP in engagements with other clients, provided Consultant does not disclose Client's confidential information or reproduce Client-specific Deliverables.

5.4 Client represents that any materials it provides to Consultant do not infringe the rights of any third party.

### 6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party may disclose confidential or proprietary information to the other ("Confidential Information"). The receiving Party shall: (a) use Confidential Information only to perform under this Agreement; (b) protect it with the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care; and (c) not disclose it to third parties except to employees, contractors, or advisors bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section.

6.2 Confidential Information does not include information that: is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party; was rightfully known before disclosure; is rightfully received from a third party without restriction; or is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

6.3 A Party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law or court order, provided it gives the other Party prompt notice where legally permitted.

6.4 This Section survives termination of this Agreement for [3 YEARS / INDEFINITELY FOR TRADE SECRETS: SPECIFY].

### 7. AI-specific terms

7.1 **Human review required.** Services may involve the use of AI Tools. Consultant reviews AI-generated output as part of the engagement, but Client is responsible for reviewing and approving any AI-assisted output before relying on it for business-critical, financial, legal, medical, safety, or regulatory decisions. Consultant does not warrant that AI Tools or AI-generated output will be accurate, complete, error-free, or fit for any particular purpose.

7.2 **Accuracy of Client Data.** Client is solely responsible for the accuracy, completeness, and legality of all Client Data it provides. Consultant is not liable for errors or omissions in Deliverables to the extent caused by inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading Client Data.

7.3 **Third-party AI dependency.** Services may rely on AI Tools operated by third parties (including OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic). Consultant does not control and is not responsible for the uptime, pricing, output behavior, policy changes, or discontinuation of any AI Tool. The terms of service of each AI Tool provider apply to the processing of any data submitted to it, and those terms flow down to Client's use of the Deliverables to the extent applicable. If a provider materially changes its terms, pricing, or availability, Consultant may substitute a comparable AI Tool with notice to Client.

7.4 **Data handling and opt-out.** In the course of Services, Client Data may be submitted to one or more AI Tools identified in the applicable SOW or the intake process for processing (for example, to draft content, analyze documents, or power an automation). Consultant will use enterprise or API-tier access for AI Tools where available, which providers generally exclude from model training by default. Client may request that specific categories of Client Data be excluded from submission to AI Tools by notifying Consultant in writing; Consultant will work with Client to identify an alternative approach, which may affect scope, timeline, or fees under the applicable SOW. Client shall not provide, and Consultant shall have no obligation to process, protected health information (PHI) or payment card data unless a separate written agreement (including any required Business Associate Agreement) is in place covering that data category.

7.5 **No professional advice.** Services and Deliverables are provided for general business, operational, and technical purposes only. Nothing Consultant provides constitutes legal, financial, tax, medical, or other licensed professional advice, and Client should not rely on it as such. Client should consult a qualified professional before making decisions in those areas.

### 8. Limitation of liability

8.1 EXCEPT FOR THE CARVE-OUTS IN SECTION 8.3, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, LOST REVENUE, OR LOST DATA, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT OR ANY SOW, REGARDLESS OF THE THEORY OF LIABILITY, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

8.2 EXCEPT FOR THE CARVE-OUTS IN SECTION 8.3, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT AND ALL SOWS, IN THE AGGREGATE, SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CLIENT TO CONSULTANT UNDER THE APPLICABLE SOW IN THE 12 MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

8.3 The limitations in Sections 8.1 and 8.2 do not apply to: (a) a Party's breach of Section 6 (Confidentiality); (b) Consultant's indemnification obligations for IP infringement arising from Deliverables Consultant creates independent of Client-supplied materials; or (c) either Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

### 9. Insurance

9.1 Consultant shall maintain, for the duration of this Agreement, professional liability (errors and omissions) insurance with limits of at least **$1,000,000 per claim and $2,000,000 in the aggregate**. Consultant shall provide a certificate of insurance upon Client's written request.

### 10. Independent contractor status

10.1 Consultant is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, joint venturer, or agent of Client. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, or agency relationship. Consultant is solely responsible for its own taxes, benefits, and insurance, and for the means and methods of performing the Services, subject to the deliverables and timelines agreed in each SOW.

### 11. Non-solicitation

11.1 During the term of this Agreement and for **12 months** after its termination, neither Party shall directly solicit for employment or engagement any employee or individual contractor of the other Party who was materially involved in the Services, without that Party's prior written consent. This Section does not restrict general job postings not targeted at the other Party's personnel, or hiring someone who responds to such a posting on their own initiative.

### 12. General provisions

12.1 **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [STATE], without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

12.2 **Dispute resolution.** [SPECIFY: e.g., the Parties will attempt good-faith negotiation for 30 days before litigation; venue in the state or federal courts located in [COUNTY, STATE]; OR binding arbitration under [RULES]: CHOOSE ONE WITH COUNSEL.]

12.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except to a successor in a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.

12.4 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by email with confirmation of receipt, or by mail to the addresses above.

12.5 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, together with all executed SOWs, is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions or agreements on that subject matter.

12.6 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written document signed by both Parties.

12.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

12.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including electronic signature, each of which is an original.
