# Outreach templates

Five templates for the marketing motion in the launch roadmap: chamber outreach, referral partners, warm follow-up, a talk abstract, and a LinkedIn connection note.

Style notes: no dashes as connectors, no marketing buzzwords, sentence-case headings, plain language, emails under 150 words, no exclamation marks, no fake urgency.

---

## 1. Chamber of commerce introduction email

Subject: Quick intro, and an idea for a member talk

Hi [NAME],

I'm Caleb Bolden, I run an AI consulting practice through Vora Technologies here in [CITY/REGION], and I'd like to get to know the chamber.

I'd welcome 20 minutes for coffee to introduce myself and hear what members are dealing with day to day. No pitch, I'm not looking to sell anything on this call.

Separately, I'd be glad to give a free talk to members on finding where AI actually pays off in a small business versus where it's just noise. Practical, no jargon, built around real workflows. If that's useful for a lunch-and-learn or a regular meeting slot, happy to work around your calendar.

Let me know what works.

## 4. Talk abstract: "Map the work first" (100-120 words)

Most small businesses get an AI pitch before anyone looks at how the work actually happens. That order is backwards, and it's a big reason so many AI projects go nowhere. This talk walks through a different starting point: mapping your real workflow, step by step, before any tool gets discussed. You'll see how to spot which steps are rule-based and easy to automate, which need a person's judgment, and which are just wasted motion no software will fix. You'll leave with a way to look at your own operation and tell the difference between an AI opportunity and a distraction, using the same process mapping used in lean manufacturing, applied to office and service work.
