# Lead qualification checklist

Run this before booking a discovery call, not during one. It's a pass/fail screen, not a scoring rubric. The point isn't to be generous, it's to keep calls off the calendar that were never going to close.

Client selection is most of what drives churn later. A client who barely passes screening today is a client who's hard to work with in month two. Screening well beats being good at convincing people who shouldn't be convinced.

## The five checks

1. **5+ Google reviews.** Fewer than that usually means the business is too early-stage to have a real customer flow worth optimizing, or doesn't have the operating history to know what's actually broken.
2. **Verified Google Business Profile listing.** A claimed, verified GBP is a proxy for a business that takes its own operations seriously enough to maintain basic infrastructure.
3. **Decision-maker on the call.** If the person booking isn't the owner or someone with real authority to sign, don't book the discovery call yet. Ask who else needs to be there first.
4. **Budget signal.** Something in the inbound message, referral context, or booking form suggests they can afford this. Not a guarantee, a signal: business size, a specific dollar problem mentioned, a referral source who already vetted them.
5. **Urgency signal.** A specific trigger, not just curiosity. A busy season coming, a hire they can't make, a mistake that already cost them money. "Just looking into AI" with no trigger is a weak lead, don't rush the call.

## How to use it

All five pass: book the call.

One or two fail: use judgment. A strong referral can cover a missing review count. A clear budget and urgency signal can cover a thin GBP. Don't auto-reject on a technicality if the context is otherwise strong.

Three or more fail: don't book. Send a short, honest note instead ("sounds like it's early for this, happy to reconnect down the line") or point them at a free resource on the site.

## Where this lives in the funnel

This checklist runs on every inbound lead and every warm referral before the discovery call goes on the calendar. It does not run on outbound cold-email targets, those get pre-screened during list-building using the same five criteria, see `cold-email-sequence.md`.
