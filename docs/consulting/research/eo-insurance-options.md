# E&O / cyber insurance options — Vora Technologies LLC (solo AI consultant)

Researched 2026-07-06 (sonnet agent, web sources cited inline). Decision-ready.

## Bottom line

For a solo consultant under $250k revenue wanting $1M per-claim / $2M
aggregate tech E&O plus cyber, expect roughly $1,200-$1,800/year
($100-150/month), possibly less quoted direct through Hiscox or Hartford.
$1M/$2M is the standard MSA ask (Cornell vendor minimums cite exactly this);
don't over-buy beyond it unless a specific contract demands more.

## The AI-exclusion landscape (why the broker questions matter)

- Historically policies were "silent" on AI. In early 2026 Chubb, Travelers,
  and Berkshire Hathaway got approval to add explicit AI exclusions; W.R.
  Berkley has an absolute AI exclusion endorsement for E&O (Form PC 51380).
- ISO endorsements CG 40 47 (broad) and CG 40 48 (narrow) took effect
  January 2026 for GL; the same trend is spreading across product lines and
  shows up in renewal packets unnoticed.
- Embroker went the other way and added affirmative AI coverage (Aug 2025).

## Cyber: bundle it

Deploying AI systems that touch client customer data makes breach exposure a
foreseeable claim path independent of bad-advice claims. Bundled tech
E&O + cyber runs $67-150/mo vs ~$81/mo for standalone cyber; bundle.

## Three questions for the broker

1. Does this policy contain an AI exclusion endorsement (anything resembling
   ISO CG 40 47/40 48 or carrier-specific like W.R. Berkley PC 51380), and
   does it apply to E&O or only GL/D&O?
2. If a client claims financial loss from AI-generated advice, an AI system
   error, or a hallucinated output delivered in an engagement, is that
   covered as professional negligence or excluded as AI-related?
3. Is affirmative AI coverage available as a named endorsement, and is there
   an AI sublimit or does it draw the full policy limit?
