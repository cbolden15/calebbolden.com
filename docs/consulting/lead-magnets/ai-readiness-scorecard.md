# Is your business ready for AI? The 18-question scorecard

*A self-assessment for owners of businesses with 2 to 50 people. Takes about 10 minutes. No technical knowledge needed. Print it or fill it in on screen.*

---

76% of small businesses now use AI in some form, but only 14% have it working inside their actual operations (Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses survey, 2026). The difference between those two numbers is not the tools. It is whether the business was ready before the tools showed up.

This scorecard tells you which side of that gap you are on, and what to do first either way.

**How it works:** answer each question Yes (2 points), Sometimes (1 point), or No (0 points). Add up each section, then your total. The bands at the end tell you what your score means.

## Your score

**Grand total: ____ / 36**

### 0 to 14: Foundations first

Do not buy an AI tool this quarter. Your money is better spent getting customer data into one place and writing down how your top two processes actually work. This is not a setback; businesses that skip this step are the ones that feed the failure statistics. Most software project failures trace to people and process, not the platform (Forrester and HBR analyses put people-related causes at over 60% of CRM failures). Start with the one-hour process audit worksheet, then reassess.

### 15 to 26: Ready for one pilot

You have enough structure to automate one thing, and only one. Look at your Section 3 answers: whichever task came up as most repetitive is your pilot. Keep a person reviewing the output for the first month. Resist the urge to roll out three tools at once; the adoption gap (76% using AI, 14% integrated) is made of businesses that scaled before one thing worked.

### 27 to 36: Ready to sequence

Your data, processes, and team can support AI across several workflows. Your risk is no longer readiness, it is ordering: which automation first, which measurements prove it worked, and when to add the next one. This is where a process-first plan pays for itself, because at your level the wins compound.
