# Get found on Google: the local SEO checklist

*20 items, ordered by impact. The one-time fixes come first, then the weekly routine that does the compounding. Written for owners, not marketers.*

---

When someone searches "plumber near me" or "best salon in [your town]," Google decides who shows up in the map pack. Its weighting is studied every year: in the 2026 Whitespark Local Search Ranking Factors survey, Google Business Profile signals carry about 32% of map-pack ranking weight, on-page website signals about 19%, and review signals continue to climb. That tells you exactly where your effort goes: profile first, website second, reviews forever.

## One-time fixes (a weekend, once)

- [ ] **1. Claim your Google Business Profile and fill in every field.** No blanks. Complete profiles get dramatically more clicks than partial ones, and this single listing outweighs everything else on this page.
- [ ] **2. Choose the most specific primary category that fits.** "HVAC contractor," not "contractor." Category choice is repeatedly measured as one of the biggest single ranking levers. Add accurate secondary categories after.
- [ ] **3. Make your business name, address, and phone number identical everywhere.** Website, Google, Yelp, Facebook, directories. Same suite number, same phone format. Inconsistent listings quietly drag down local visibility.
- [ ] **4. Hunt down old listings with a previous address or number and fix or remove them.** Search your business name plus your old address to find them.
- [ ] **5. Upload 10 to 15 real photos to your profile now.** Storefront, team, work in progress, finished jobs. Businesses with photos get 42% more direction requests and 35% more website clicks (Google's own data), and top-ranked local businesses carry far larger photo libraries than page-two ones.
- [ ] **6. Enter your services individually in the services section with descriptions.** Not just a sentence in your business description.
- [ ] **7. Set accurate hours, including holidays.** Wrong hours are one of the fastest ways to turn a ready buyer into a competitor's customer.
- [ ] **8. Give each core service and each town you serve its own page on your website.** On-page signals are the second-largest ranking factor, and one generic homepage cannot rank for ten services in five towns.
- [ ] **9. Put your city and service in your page titles and headings, written naturally.** "Water heater repair in Bentonville" as a heading beats "Our Services."
- [ ] **10. Put your full address, service area, and an embedded Google map on your contact page.**
- [ ] **11. Get listed in the directories that matter for your industry.** Chamber of commerce, BBB, and your trade's directories, with the identical name-address-phone from item 3.

## The weekly routine (1 to 2 hours, forever)

- [ ] **12. Ask every happy customer for a Google review, the same day.** Recency now matters more than raw count in ranking studies: a steady weekly trickle beats an occasional burst. Make the ask part of closing the job.
- [ ] **13. Respond to every review within a day or two, positive and negative.** 88% of consumers prefer businesses that respond to all reviews, and businesses with high response rates measurably outrank low responders (BrightLocal 2025; Whitespark 2026).
- [ ] **14. Never buy, trade for, or fake reviews.** Three quarters of consumers actively watch for fakes, and Google removes profiles for it. Not worth it in any universe.
- [ ] **15. Post to your profile once or twice a week.** An offer, a finished job with photo, a seasonal tip. Each post takes under 10 minutes, and profiles that look alive are rewarded because engagement signals keep climbing in the ranking studies.
- [ ] **16. Add a couple of fresh photos each month.**
- [ ] **17. Turn on messaging and answer the Q&A section.** Questions sitting unanswered on your profile are being answered by strangers instead.
- [ ] **18. Add one genuinely useful page or update to your website monthly.** A common question answered properly, a project write-up, a seasonal checklist for customers.

## Keep score (monthly, 10 minutes)

- [ ] **19. Check your profile's calls, direction requests, and website clicks in the performance tab.** These are the numbers your weekly routine should move. Write them down monthly.
- [ ] **20. Review your profile quarterly for changes you didn't make.** Google auto-applies suggested edits (hours, categories) from the public, and they can silently overwrite you.

## What to expect

Local SEO compounds, it does not spike. The typical pattern across practitioner data: months 1 to 3 are groundwork with little visible movement, months 3 to 6 show map-pack gains, and months 6 to 12 are where the phone measurably rings more. Low-competition markets move faster, sometimes inside 60 days. If someone promises page one in two weeks, keep your wallet in your pocket.
