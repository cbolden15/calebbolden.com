# Client intake questionnaire

Sent after the first call, before the discovery week starts.

*Send with: "Fill this out whenever you have 15 minutes this week. There's no wrong answer, especially the ones that make you wince. The more honest this is, the less time we waste during the interviews."*

---

## Before you start

I'm asking these questions so the discovery week starts with real information instead of guesses. It also means I'm not spending your team's interview time asking things you could've told me in five minutes on a form. Answer in your own words. Short answers are fine.

Takes about 15 minutes.

---

## Section 1: The business

1. What's the business, in one or two sentences, like you'd explain it to a stranger at a barbecue?
2. Industry / trade:
3. How many people work here (owners, employees, regular contractors)?
   - [ ] Just me / 1-2 people
   - [ ] 3-10
   - [ ] 11-25
   - [ ] 26-50
   - [ ] 50+
4. What does a good month look like? (More jobs, more revenue, fewer fires, something else. Be specific if you can.)
5. What does a bad month look like, and what usually causes it?

## Section 2: The tools that run the place

6. What do you use to track customers and jobs (CRM, spreadsheet, notebook, memory)?
7. What do you use for scheduling?
8. What do you use for invoicing and getting paid?
9. What do you use for bookkeeping or accounting (QuickBooks, an accountant who has it all, a shoebox)?
10. How do customers usually reach you first? (Phone, text, website form, walk-in, referral call, Facebook, other)
11. Do you use text messaging or email for customer updates, or is it mostly phone calls?
12. Any tool on this list held together by a spreadsheet and someone's memory? Which one?
13. Is there a piece of software you paid for and stopped using? What happened?

## Section 3: Where the time goes

14. What are the three tasks that eat the most time in a normal week?
15. What's the one task you'd pay to never do again?
16. What's something that only gets done at night or on a weekend because there's no time for it during the day?
17. When something goes wrong with a job or a customer, who finds out first, and how?
18. Where do you (or your team) end up typing the same information into two different places?

## Section 4: What's been tried

19. Have you tried automating anything before (a CRM, a chatbot, Zapier, an AI tool, anything)? What happened?
20. If yes: what made it stick, or what made it die?
21. Has anyone on the team already built their own workaround for something the "official" system doesn't handle well? What is it?

## Section 5: Data reality

22. Where does customer information actually live right now? (One system, three systems, a filing cabinet, all of the above)
23. If I asked for last month's numbers (jobs done, revenue, whatever you track) right now, would you trust the report, or would you want to double-check it by hand first?

## Section 6: Logistics

24. Who makes the final call on spending and process changes here?
25. Who else should I talk to this week? (Name and role. Aim for the owner plus 3-5 people who touch the work daily: office staff, a lead tech or manager, someone on the front line.)
26. Any days or times this week that are off-limits for interviews?
27. What's the timeline pressure, if any? (Busy season coming, a hire falling through, nothing urgent, etc.)

---

That's it. Send it back whenever it's done, and I'll come into the interviews already knowing the shape of the business instead of starting from zero.
