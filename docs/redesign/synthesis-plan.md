## Decision: full rebrand

The current site is treated as inspiration only. Palette, type, and layout are all open.
Positioning and copy stay. No purple.

## Direction: minimal-clean

Chosen by pool depth, not taste alone. The library can only support a full homepage in
two differentiating directions:

| Vibe | hero | features | cta | nav | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bold-energetic | 6 | 0 | 1 | 0 | Too thin |
| warm-aspirational | 12 | 5 | 1 | 1 | Thin on cta/nav |
| editorial-stark | 9 | 6 | 3 | 4 | Viable, bolder, thinner |
| **minimal-clean** | **43** | **19** | **10** | **18** | **Chosen — deep support** |
| dark-premium | 96 | 18 | 20 | 35 | This is the current look; rebrand moves off it |

`minimal-clean` reads trustworthy and human for small business owners, is a hard pivot off
the dark look, and the library can fully deliver it. `editorial-stark` is the bolder,
more typographic alternative if the team wants it.

## Palette shift

Dark `#030712` base → light base (off-white / white), near-black ink text, a warm
sunrise-gradient accent for humanity, optional single cool accent. No purple.

## Combined intent

A bright, confident, human consultancy. Large calm whitespace and a strong mixed-weight
headline carry authority. One framed video card anchors the hero. Warm gradient accents
keep it from feeling sterile or corporate. The site should read like a trusted operator
who gives you your time back, not another AI tool.
