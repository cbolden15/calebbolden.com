About

Where details become experiences.

Ogechi built her career on the belief that hospitality is a form of respect — that the smallest details, done with care, are what people remember. A pharmacist by training, she brings the same precision to a welcome suite as she once did to a prescription.

Between clinical work, brand partnerships, and building The OJÄ Experience, she still makes time to write, host, and answer the questions people are too shy to ask anyone else.
