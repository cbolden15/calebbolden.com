The foundation

Two things make a website yours.

The domain is the address. Hosting and website care keep everything available, secure, and working.

01

Your domain

This is the address people type, such as madamgech.com. It renews each year. If it expires, another person can register it.

02

Hosting and care

Hosting is where the website lives. Website care covers updates, backups, security, monitoring, and repairs.

Keep every domain in an account under your own name, email, and payment card. That remains true even when Caleb manages everything else.

Choose the address

Start with the name.

The new site is a complete personal brand site, with Madam Koverage preserved as its blog rather than the whole identity.

  • First pick madamgech.com Short, memorable, and aligned with your Instagram identity.
  • Second pick drmadamgech.com Keeps the same identity and adds your professional title.
  • Third pick themadamgech.com A clear fallback if the shorter addresses are taken.
  • Blog-led option themadamkoverage.com Best only if you want the blog name to remain the main identity.

These names were checked on September 6, 2026. Availability can change until a domain is registered. Choose a .com, use your own account, and turn on automatic renewal.

Choose the arrangement

Manage it yourself, or hand it off.

Both choices keep the domains and website content in your name. The difference is who takes responsibility for the technical work.

Choice A: self-managed

Open your own Hostinger account.

Lower cost You manage the account

Choose Hostinger Premium with a 12-month term. The current Premium plan supports three websites, which is enough for both projects. You handle billing, renewals, support, and routine maintenance.

  1. Open WordPress Hosting

    Visit Hostinger and choose its managed hosting for WordPress.

  2. Select Premium

    Choose the least expensive plan that supports at least two websites.

  3. Choose 12 months

    A longer term lowers the advertised monthly price but requires more money upfront.

  4. Remove extras

    Untick site builders, marketing tools, and other products you did not request.

  5. Claim the included domain

    Annual plans currently include one domain for the first year. Confirm the discount before paying.

  6. Create your account

    Use your own email, card, and a password you do not use anywhere else.

  7. Review the total

    Prices change with promotions. Confirm the term, renewal price, taxes, and final total.

  8. Share access safely

    Open Account Sharing and invite Caleb. Do not send your account password.

Choice B: fully managed

Let Caleb manage both websites.

$69 Per month, both sites

You own the domains and content. Caleb takes responsibility for the hosting and the routine technical work behind both websites.

  • Hosting, security certificates, and domain connections
  • Daily off-site backups and uptime monitoring
  • WordPress, theme, and plugin updates
  • Troubleshooting and recovery when something breaks
  • One small content-update batch each month, up to 30 minutes shared between both sites

Support requests receive a response within two business days. New pages, redesigns, copywriting, large content changes, domain renewals, and paid software licenses are separate. The rate is fixed for the first 12 months, then reviewed annually. You can cancel with 30 days' notice and receive a complete copy of both websites.

What to send Caleb.

If you self-manage

Send the domain and account invite.

Register the domain, purchase Hostinger, then invite Caleb through Account Sharing. Keep your password private.

Register the domains in your name, approve the monthly agreement, and Caleb will handle the hosting and setup.
