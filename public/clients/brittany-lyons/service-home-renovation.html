Full home renovations in Dallas–Fort Worth.

A full home renovation keeps your home's footprint and reworks everything inside it: layout, finishes, lighting, and built-ins, without adding square footage. For most DFW families that means a home that fits how you live now, not how it was built decades ago. I take the project from the first walk-through to the day you move back in, so you get one designer who knows the whole thing rather than a handoff between people.

Cavender · Oak Cliff, TX · Renovation + Addition · 2025

One designer, the whole project.

Renovations suit homes with good bones that need to work harder, feel calmer, and look like you. Here's what I handle so you don't have to hold it all at once.

·

Space planning & layout

Reworking how rooms flow and function for the way your household actually moves through the day.

·

Finishes & materials

Flooring, tile, counters, cabinetry, paint, and hardware, selected as one coherent palette.

·

Lighting & fixtures

Light layered for how a room is used, plus plumbing fixtures chosen to match the finishes.

·

Built-ins & window treatments

Custom cabinetry, storage, and workroom-made window coverings measured to each room.

·

Trade coordination

Project leadership from demolition through the final install, working with your trades or mine.
