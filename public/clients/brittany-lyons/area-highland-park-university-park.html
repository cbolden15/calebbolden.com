Home/Service Areas/Highland Park & University Park

Interior designer in the Park Cities.

Highland Park and University Park are two of the closer-in, more established communities I work with, known for traditional and Tudor-influenced estate homes on tighter, walkable lots. Both cities run their own architectural review for exterior work, so a project here usually starts with understanding what's allowed before we start drawing anything.

Traditional homes, updated living.

The housing stock in Highland Park and University Park leans traditional: symmetrical facades, formal rooms, older floor plans that weren't built for how most families use a house today. The interior work I do here is often less about tearing walls out and more about reworking how those existing rooms function, updating finishes, and bringing in furnishings that respect the architecture instead of fighting it.

Because both cities have architectural review processes, I plan for that timeline early, whether the project needs board approval or is purely interior and doesn't. Either way, you'll know what to expect before we're deep into it.
