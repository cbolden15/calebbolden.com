I'm one week late, but One Room Challenge I'm here!

One Room Challenge Week One

Ok, first things first…I’m behind schedule!

I haven’t even announced on here (or socials yet) that I’m participating in the One Room Challenge! YAY!

I was supposed to have this blog post out last week and here I am…7 days behind, releasing week one when I’m supposed to be sharing about week two!

Anywho, here are the deets of this year’s one room challenge. As usual, I’m overshooting with ambition. I have plans for a kitchen, primary bath, secondary bath, complete new construction of a powder bath, primary bedroom and living room. Whew! Y’all, we only have eight weeks to get this thing wrapped up and let’s just say I need your prayers.

While I only have two months to do the work, the design has been months in the works. There are also several sponsors on board that I’ll be announcing soon. So here is the run down of how these next eight blogs will go:

Week 1 (this post you’re reading) - Intro and Secondary Powder Bath Design Board Week 2 (the next post that’s really supposed to be today) - Demo progress…spoiler alert, it’s already started! Week 3 - Kitchen Design Week 4 - Living Room Design Week 5 - Mid Challenge Progress Report Week 6 - Primary Bath Design Week 7 - Primary Bedroom

Week 8 - Final Reveal
