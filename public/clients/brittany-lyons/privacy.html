Privacy policy.

This page explains what information this website collects and how it's used. Last updated 2026.

What we collect

When you fill out the contact form on this site, we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, phone number, and message. We also use standard website analytics, which record general information about visits, such as pages viewed and the type of device used. Analytics does not include your name or contact details unless you've given them to us directly through a form.

How we use it

Contact form details are used to respond to your inquiry, whether that's scheduling a discovery call or answering a question about a project. Analytics is used to understand how people use the site and where it can be improved. We don't use your information for anything beyond these purposes.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information to anyone. We don't share contact form details outside the studio except where noted below.

Third-party services

This site relies on a small number of outside providers to operate: a form-handling service to deliver contact form submissions by email, an email provider to receive and respond to those messages, and an analytics provider to measure site traffic. Each of these providers processes data under its own privacy practices, and none of them are permitted to use your information for their own marketing.

Cookies

The site may use cookies or similar technology as part of standard analytics, mainly to distinguish one visit from another. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Your choices

If you'd like your information corrected or removed from our records, or you have any question about what we hold, email info@brittanylyonsinteriors.com and we'll take care of it directly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the current version.
