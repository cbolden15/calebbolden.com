New Year, New Us!

Happy Friday!

If you’re in Dallas/DFW area today, you’re probably at home with the heat blasting and snacking. Well at least that’s what I’m doing. I wanted to create this blog to let you know that we have rebranded. Our name has changed from Brittany Lyons Art and Interiors, LLC to Brittany Lyons Interiors.

The main reason for this change was to shorten the name and create a brand that focuses more on who I am as a business owner and what my business has become over the years. While I still love painting, I don’t have the time to create like I want. My projects these days consist mostly of design services and not art commissions. This name change reflects that.

In addition to the name change, I had a new website created by JPW Design Studio, and a new logo created by Laura Ciel Designs. They both did such an amazing job and the logo, and website, look exactly how I wanted them to look. I feel this new website, and logo, will attract the future clients that I’d like to work with. They are also pretty to look at which is a reflection of how spaces are once my team and I complete a project. You can view the new site here.

Whether you are a client, friend or follower, I want to express my deep gratitude for your support. Being an entrepreneur is no easy feat, and there are many challenging and stressful days. What keeps me going is the continuous support that I have from all of you, and the end result of transforming a space from chaotic, or boring, into a peaceful, functional design.

Thanks for reading my first blog on the new site! I’m going to try and be consistent in 2022 with posting a blog the first Friday of each month. If you have any topics that you’d like for me to discuss, please let me know in the comments below. Until next time friend.

xoxo,

Brittany
