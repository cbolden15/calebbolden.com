ORC Week 4: Living Room Design

ORC Week 4: Living Room

Happy November!

It’s week four and time is flying by. We are continuing to make progress in each area, but this week’s blog post focuses on the living room! Here is the design board:

Living Room Design Board

We are going for a cozy space to hang out with many seating options. There will be colors and textures throughout the space that complement the fireplace and tie the room together. With the style of the home being modern coastal with global influence, I wanted to keep it bright and airy with the paint. The color and pattern will be brought it through textiles.
