The Ultimate Guide to Home Updates Every Homeowner Needs (Because Your Home Deserves a Little TLC)

We’ve all been there. You’re sitting in your living room, sipping on your coffee (or wine, no judgment here), and you glance around. Suddenly, it hits you — “This place needs a serious makeover.” Maybe it’s that sad, outdated wallpaper from 2005, or perhaps it’s that kitchen that still has the same avocado green fridge from the '80s. Whatever it is, the itch for a home update is real, and you're ready to scratch it.

But what exactly should you focus on? What are the most popular home updates homeowners are adding that are guaranteed to up your home’s value and aesthetic? Buckle up, because we’re about to dive into the world of home updates, and trust me, it’s going to be a fun ride.

1. The Kitchen Remodel: Not Just for Food Prep, But for Serious 'Wow' Factor

Renovated kitchen from our Haskell project.

Ah, the kitchen. The heart of the home. It’s where all the magic happens, from crafting a five-star meal (which, let’s be real, is mostly microwaving leftovers) to gathering for family dinners. It’s no wonder the kitchen is often the first place homeowners focus when updating their space.

Why Is It Popular? The kitchen is the focal point of your home. A modernized kitchen not only makes cooking and entertaining more enjoyable but also dramatically increases the value of your home. So, if you're dreaming of hosting dinner parties where you’re not ashamed of your countertops, it’s time to consider a remodel.

Popular Kitchen Updates:

  • Quartz Countertops: Move over, granite. Quartz is here, and it’s sleek, low-maintenance, and stylish. Plus, it’s non-porous, so your spaghetti sauce stains will be a thing of the past.

  • Cabinet Overhaul: Goodbye, old-fashioned wood cabinets. Hello, custom cabinetry, open shelving, or those super cool matte black finishes everyone is raving about.

  • High-End Appliances: If your oven is older than your Netflix password, it’s time for an upgrade. Smart refrigerators, energy-efficient dishwashers, and Wi-Fi-enabled stoves are the way to go.

  • Backsplash Update: A chic, modern backsplash can add that little extra pop of style. Plus, it’s a fun DIY project if you’re feeling brave.

2. Bathroom Makeovers: Say Goodbye to the Dingy, Hello to Spa-like Luxury

Bathroom renovation from our Holstein project.

Let’s talk about bathrooms. Whether you’re using it to get ready for the day or retreating to relax after a long one, your bathroom should feel like an oasis. If your bathroom still has that tile from the '70s or a shower that makes you question your life choices, it’s time for an upgrade.

Why Is It Popular? Bathrooms are one of the most important rooms when it comes to home value. A modern bathroom can seriously up your home’s appeal and give it a luxurious feel. Plus, after a long day, who doesn’t want to sink into a relaxing bath or enjoy a shower that doesn’t feel like an interrogation?

Popular Bathroom Updates:

  • Freestanding Bathtubs: Is there anything more luxurious than a soaking tub? You can add one to your bathroom for an instant spa-like feel.

  • Walk-In Showers: No one has time for those tiny, cramped bathtubs anymore. Upgrade to a walk-in shower with sleek, modern tiles and a rain showerhead.

  • Modern Fixtures: Stainless steel and matte black faucets are all the rage. Out with the brass, in with the contemporary.

  • Vanity Overhaul: That basic, builder-grade vanity you’re rocking? Yeah, it’s time for a chic, stylish upgrade. Floating vanities and sleek mirrors are top trends for a reason.

3. Flooring Updates: Because Your Feet Deserve the Best

Newly updated flooring in our Cavender project.

Let’s face it — flooring is often overlooked. You might not notice it right away, but your flooring is crucial in setting the tone for your entire space. If you're still living with shag carpet from the '90s (or, even worse, linoleum), it’s time to rethink your choices.

Why Is It Popular? Flooring is a game-changer when it comes to home updates. New floors can instantly transform the look and feel of any room. Whether you’re upgrading the entire house or just focusing on key areas, investing in high-quality, durable flooring can elevate your space like nothing else.

Popular Flooring Updates:

  • Hardwood Floors: Timeless, elegant, and durable. Hardwood floors never go out of style and can fit into almost any type of interior design.

  • Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP): If you’re looking for something that looks like hardwood but is cheaper and more durable, LVP is the way to go. Plus, it’s easy to install!

  • Tile Flooring: Tile is no longer just for bathrooms. High-quality tiles can create stunning, low-maintenance floors in living rooms, kitchens, and even entryways.

Updating your home doesn’t have to be overwhelming. If you’re looking for a pro, we’d love to be considered. Brittany Lyons Interiors would love to help you curate a space that will live well with you and your family. Whether you start small with a fresh coat of paint or dive in with a full kitchen remodel, these popular updates can give your space the transformation it deserves. The best part? Most of these updates not only improve the look and feel of your home but also increase its value. So go ahead, invest in your space — your future self (and future buyer) will thank you.
