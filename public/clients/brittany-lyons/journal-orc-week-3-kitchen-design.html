ORC Week 3: Kitchen Design

Hey guys!

Week three’s blog is going to be short and sweet again. We are about 2, maybe 2 and a half weeks into construction of the project.

Here are a few progress photos of the work.

This week’s blog is going to be focusing on our kitchen design which you can see below.

I have the kitchen design board below. With this project I will be sharing all of the links, so you can find those below as well.

The goal for this kitchen is to be more open than what it originally was. We are going to do that by having custom cabinetry in a white oak wood so that it will warm up the space because our walls will be a Sherman Williams Snowbound. I think it will be a nice contrast.

We are going to do a marble slab for the counters and we’re also going to have a really cool breakfast nook bench which will be painted in a different color. We will also have some cabinetry that will go on the right side near the garage door entrance.

I am looking forward to showing you all the final for that.
