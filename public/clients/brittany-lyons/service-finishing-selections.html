Finishing selections in Dallas–Fort Worth.

Finishing selections cover paint color, flooring, and custom window treatments, the finishes that touch nearly every room in a house. Chosen separately, they tend to fight each other. I select them as one plan and measure each window in person for treatments made to the exact size. Whole-home and multi-room plans are welcome; focused requests are considered when they meet the service minimum.

Cavender · Oak Cliff, TX · Paint, Flooring & Window Treatments

Three finishes, one plan.

Paint, flooring, and window treatments are usually decided in separate visits to separate stores. Here's how I bring them together instead.

·

Paint color consulting

A whole-house palette tested against your actual light, room by room, not one swatch under store lighting.

·

Flooring selections

Wood, tile, or stone chosen to hold up to how the room is used and to read consistently room to room.

·

Custom window treatments

Each window measured in person, with drapery and hardware made to fit, not resized off the shelf.

·

Install coordination

Scheduling painters, flooring installers, and the workroom so the finishes land in the right order.

Good to know

Finishing selections can be a focused engagement when the number of spaces and requested selections meet the service minimum. We'll confirm scope on the discovery call.

I measure each window in person, then work with a workroom to have treatments made to those exact measurements, so they hang and function correctly rather than being sized off the shelf.

I select the colors and materials and can bring in trusted painters and flooring installers I work with regularly, or coordinate with a crew you already have.

Whole-home and multi-room finish plans are welcome. A single room is considered when the combination of paint, flooring, and window-treatment work meets the service minimum.
