Project Haskell.

A partial-home design that gives each gathering space its own character while keeping the bar, kitchen, dining room, living room, and fireplace connected.

Project Haskell · Partial Home · Kitchen + Living

Distinct rooms with one shared rhythm.

The rooms in Project Haskell are open to one another, but they did not need to look identical. The design gives the bar its confident blue cabinetry and teal drapery, then lets the kitchen and dining areas breathe with lighter finishes.

In the living spaces, soft upholstery and natural textures settle around the stone fireplace. The powder bath carries the same attention into a smaller footprint, using pattern and contrast without breaking the calm of the rest of the home.

The common thread is balance: expressive color where it earns attention, quieter materials where the family gathers, and sight lines that make the connected rooms feel intentional from every angle.
