Home/Service Areas/Dallas & Oak Cliff

Interior designer in Dallas & Oak Cliff.

Oak Cliff and the neighborhoods around central Dallas hold some of the area's oldest housing stock: homes built when rooms were smaller, closed off, and organized around a formal front-of-house. Most of my work here is renovation, taking a house with real character and reworking the parts that no longer fit how a family actually lives, without losing the parts that made you buy it in the first place.

Cavender · Oak Cliff, TX · Renovation + Addition · 2025

Renovation-friendly bones.

The Cavender project came to me as a full renovation and addition on an Oak Cliff home with good structure but a layout that no longer worked: closed-off rooms, a kitchen cut off from the rest of the house, and a footprint that needed to grow to fit the family living in it. That's a common starting point out here.

Dallas proper covers a wider mix, from established neighborhoods with mid-century ranches to newer infill construction. Whichever it is, I stay close to every finish and layout decision myself, because a renovation on an older home usually turns up something the original drawings didn't account for, and someone needs to be able to make a call on the spot.
