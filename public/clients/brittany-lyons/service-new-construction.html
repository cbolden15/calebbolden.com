New construction interior design in Dallas–Fort Worth.

New construction interior design means the finishes, layout input, and selections for a home that is still being built. Instead of choosing from a builder's standard options, you get a designer working through the plan set with you: where the light switches land, which way the kitchen faces, what the cabinetry and counters will be. I step in as early as the floor plan allows and stay through the final walk-through, coordinating with your builder so decisions get made before they become expensive to change.

Built right the first time.

A new build is the one chance to get the layout and finishes right before anything is behind drywall. Here's what I handle alongside your builder.

·

Plan review & layout input

Walking the floor plan before it locks, so outlets, sightlines, and traffic flow work for your household.

·

Finish & material selections

Flooring, cabinetry, counters, and tile chosen as one palette instead of separate showroom decisions.

·

Lighting & plumbing fixtures

A lighting plan set before rough-in, plus fixtures selected to match the rest of the home.

·

Window treatments & styling

Custom window coverings and furnishing plans ready to install as soon as you close.

·

Builder coordination

Attending selection meetings and keeping design decisions on schedule with your builder's timeline.

Good to know

A builder's in-house designer usually works from a set list of options and covers several buyers at once. I work only for you, so the selections reflect your taste and how you actually live, not what moves fastest through the builder's schedule.

As early as possible, ideally before the floor plan is finalized. Once framing and rough-in are set, some layout changes become expensive or impossible. Early involvement lets me weigh in on outlet placement, lighting plans, and cabinetry before it is locked.
