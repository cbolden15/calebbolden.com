Bathroom design in Dallas–Fort Worth.

Bathroom design covers the layout, tile, lighting, storage, and fixtures for a primary bath, guest bath, or powder room. A bathroom is a small footprint asked to do a lot, so I plan the layout first: where the vanity, shower, and storage sit relative to each other, then choose tile and fixtures that hold up daily and still feel calm. Some projects are a full reconfiguration with moved plumbing, others are a finishes-only refresh. Either way I plan the waterproofing and trade sequence before any tile goes up.

Good to know

Bathroom cost depends heavily on scope: a powder room refresh runs far less than reworking a primary bath's plumbing layout. We start with a complimentary discovery call and a $350 in-home consultation, then I put together pricing for your specific project. More detail is on the investment page.

A powder room can move in a few weeks. A primary bath with a new layout or moved plumbing usually takes a few months once you factor in tile and fixture lead times and the trade schedule.

A primary bath typically involves a shower, tub, and full plumbing layout, so it takes longer and costs more. A powder room is smaller and often just needs finishes, lighting, and a vanity, so it moves faster.

I design the layout, tile, and fixtures, and I coordinate the trades responsible for waterproofing, plumbing, and tile setting, whether that's your contractor or one I work with regularly.
