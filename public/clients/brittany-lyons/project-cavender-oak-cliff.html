Cavender.

A full home renovation and addition for a family who needed their Oak Cliff house to catch up with them: more room to gather, and a layout that finally made sense for the way they actually move through a day.

Cavender · Oak Cliff, TX · Renovation + Addition · 2025

A house that no longer fit the family.

Like a lot of Oak Cliff homes, this one had good structure and a layout that had stopped serving the people living in it. Rooms were disconnected from each other, storage was scattered, and there wasn't a clear place for the household to actually gather.

The renovation and addition addressed both problems at once: reworking the existing footprint room by room, and adding the square footage the family needed for the way they wanted to use the house day to day. Every selection, from flooring to lighting to hardware, was chosen as one palette rather than room by room, so the finished home reads as a single calm idea instead of a series of separate updates.

The result is a home that looks like it was always meant to work this way: more light, easier flow between rooms, and a quiet, cohesive palette that doesn't compete with the family living inside it.
