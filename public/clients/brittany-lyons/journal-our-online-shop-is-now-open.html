Our online shop is now OPEN!

Hi everyone!

August is officially here, and I have an exciting announcement to share. My online shop is officially LIVE! For so long I have wanted to create an online shop of my own, where I could sell items from past projects, offer a custom candle (SPOILER ALERT!) and curate pieces that truly reflect my personal design style. It’s been in the making for a long time, but it’s finally here! I am thrilled to have these piece for your to select from and new items will be added monthly. To visit the shop, you can click here.

Another exciting announcement is the launch of our custom candle Restore. Restore was created with luxurious citrus scents to help you rehabilitate your mind after a long day of who knows what. When I tell you this candle is luxury, it is just that. The smell is unbelievable.

If there are any specific items that you are needing for your home, and would you’d like to see on the shop, let me know! I’m so grateful that I will be able to serve you further in this capacity! Until next time.

On another note, I have a design course for beginner designer’s that will be launching soon. If you, or someone you know, have interest. Please reach out to me via email to find out more!

xoxo,

Brittany
