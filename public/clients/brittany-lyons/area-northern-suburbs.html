Interior designer across the northern suburbs.

Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Allen, and Aubrey are among the fastest-growing parts of DFW, and a good share of the homes I see here are newer builds or houses still under construction. That means a lot of my work is selections: getting in early with your builder to choose finishes, fixtures, and layouts before they're locked into a standard package. Lots tend to get larger the further north and east you go, especially around Aubrey.

Sukay · Dallas–Fort Worth

Design before you build.

When a home is still on paper, the opportunity is different from a renovation. Instead of working around what already exists, we're choosing it: countertops, cabinetry, plumbing fixtures, flooring, lighting, and how rooms relate to each other, all before a single selection gets locked in by a builder's default package.

That's the kind of work represented by Sukay Drive in Dallas–Fort Worth, and it's a familiar shape for families building across Plano, Frisco, Allen, and further out toward Aubrey, where lots run larger and layouts have more room to work with. I coordinate directly with your builder so the finishes read as one considered plan instead of a list of upgrades.
