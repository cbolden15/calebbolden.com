Project Vickery.

A Spanish-inspired kitchen renovation inside an existing footprint: a calmer palette, warm finishes, and choices tuned to how this household actually spends its time at home.

Vickery · Spanish-Inspired Kitchen Renovation · 2025

Good bones, tired finishes.

A kitchen renovation can change how the whole home feels, even when the footprint already works. What needed attention here was the mix of finishes, furniture, and color that had accumulated over the years without ever being considered as a whole.

Here that meant stepping back and treating the kitchen as part of one calmer palette: warmer tones, furnishings that actually suit how the space gets used, and finishes that read as intentional rather than inherited. Nothing structural changed, but the feeling of the rooms did.

The finished space is calmer to be in and easier to keep that way, which was the point from the first conversation.
