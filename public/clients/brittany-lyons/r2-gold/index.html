The studio

Hi! I'm Brittany. I run a boutique interior design firm that's been serving clients in the Dallas, Texas area since 2016.

One of my clients came to me in the middle of a transition. She was living alone for the first time, in a house that did not yet feel like hers. We made it into a sanctuary, quiet enough to rest in and warm enough to come home to. She told me later that the rooms had changed how her days started.

That is the work. Everyone deserves a personal sanctuary for comfort and restoration, and a house can be one if it is planned with care.

How we can help

Full home renovations, additions, and ground-up new construction. We work alongside your builder from plans through the final install, so the finishes, lighting and millwork are decided before they are ordered.

The discovery call is free. An in-home consultation is $350. Pricing after that is set by project.

For rooms that are structurally fine and simply are not working. New furniture plans, sourcing and styling, using what is worth keeping and replacing what is not.

Painting selections, flooring selections, and custom window treatments, specified as a set so the whole house reads as one house.
