Kitchen design in Dallas–Fort Worth.

Kitchen design covers the layout, cabinetry, counters, lighting, and storage that turn a kitchen into the room your family actually gathers in. I start by talking through how you cook, how many people are usually in the room at once, and what currently gets in the way, then plan the space around that instead of around a showroom display. From there I select every finish as one palette and can lead the project through construction, whether that means new cabinetry and a reworked layout or a full gut to the studs.

Vickery · Spanish-Inspired Kitchen Renovation · 2025

A kitchen built around how you live.

The kitchen carries more daily use than any other room in the house. Here's what I handle to make sure it holds up to that.

·

Layout & workflow

Placing the sink, range, and prep space so cooking and cleanup don't compete for the same square footage.

·

Cabinetry & storage

Custom or semi-custom cabinets planned around what actually needs to be stored, not a standard layout.

·

Counters, backsplash & hardware

Stone, tile, and hardware selected together so the finishes read as one considered kitchen.

·

Lighting & fixtures

Task lighting over counters, ambient lighting for the room, and plumbing fixtures to match.

·

Contractor coordination

Working with your contractor or mine to keep the build on schedule from demo through install.

Good to know

Kitchens vary more than any other room, since cabinetry and counters can range widely in price. We start with a complimentary discovery call and a $350 in-home consultation, then I put together pricing once we've walked the space and talked through scope. More detail is on the investment page.

Design and selections typically take a few weeks to a couple of months, depending on how much of the layout is changing. Cabinetry and stone lead times usually set the pace for construction after that.
