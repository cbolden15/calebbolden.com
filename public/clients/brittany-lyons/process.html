How it works

Five steps from first call to the reveal.

Every project moves through the same five stages. I keep it that way on purpose, so you always know what's happening and what comes next.

01

Discovery Call

Complimentary. Tell me about your home, what's working and what isn't, and where you'd like it to go. There's no obligation to continue after we talk.

Complimentary
02

In-Home Consultation

$350. I walk your space in person and we shape the scope of the project together, room by room, so pricing reflects the actual work ahead.

$350
03

Design & Planning

Design boards are ready roughly four to six weeks after we go under contract. For major renovations or new construction, renderings are optional and typically $300–$500.

~4–6 wks
04

Procurement

Furniture, fixtures, and materials are ordered, inspected on arrival, and stored at our Dallas warehouse until your install date. Nothing sits in your house waiting.

Ongoing
05

Construction & Install

Trades are coordinated and scheduled so everything comes together on install day. This is the stage that ends in your reveal.
