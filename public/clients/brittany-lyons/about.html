Home/About

Design that helps a home feel like rest.

I'm Brittany Lyons, owner and principal designer. I began designing spaces for client homes in 2015 and established my Dallas studio in 2019.

Soft-spoken design,
seriously made.

My parents flipped houses and managed rental properties while I was growing up, so homes in progress were always part of the background. Design became personal after college, when I decorated my first apartment and shared it online. A friend saw the photos and asked me to select new furnishings and decor for her new home. That first request in 2015 changed the trajectory of my life.

I moved to Dallas in 2016, kept taking projects through word of mouth, and officially established the studio here in 2019.

Today I focus on full renovations, new construction, kitchens, primary bathrooms, and select redesigns. I stay close to every project because a boutique studio only works when the person you hired remains involved. The studio serves DFW and accepts travel projects when the scope and schedule align.

"Some rooms simply function. Yours should live well with you."

Brittany · Owner & Principal Designer
