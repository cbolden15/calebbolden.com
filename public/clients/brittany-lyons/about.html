Home/About

Design that helps a home feel like rest.

I'm Brittany Lyons. I've run a small residential design studio in Dallas since 2016, working closely with families on the homes they actually live in.

Soft-spoken design,
seriously made.

Most of my work starts the same way. We sit down and talk about how you move through your day: where the light lands in the morning, which rooms you tend to avoid, what "finished" would actually feel like for you.

From there I take on the whole house or one room at a time. Full renovations, ground-up builds, and redesigns of homes that already have good bones. I stay close to every selection, because a boutique studio only works if the person you hired is the person doing the work.

Beautiful matters to me. Whether your home gives you a little more calm at the end of the day matters just as much. Everyone deserves a place that feels like their own, made for comfort and for rest.

"Some rooms simply function. Yours should restore you."

Brittany · Owner & Principal Designer
