Sukay.

A Dallas–Fort Worth home built around a simple question: what would make this family want to spend more time here, together, without the house feeling like it's trying too hard.

Sukay · Dallas–Fort Worth · Featured Project

A home for everyday use.

This project started the way most do: a walk through the house, talking through which rooms got used and which ones didn't, and what was getting in the way of the second group.

From there the work focused on the pieces that shape daily life the most, the finishes, lighting, and layout decisions that make a room easy to actually be in rather than just look at. Nothing here was chosen to photograph well first; it was chosen to hold up to a family using it every day.

What came out of it is a home with a lighter, more consistent feel room to room, and spaces that finally get used the way they were meant to.
