Home/Services/Redesigns

Interior redesign in Dallas–Fort Worth.

A redesign works with the home you already have. No walls move, no permits, no construction crew. Instead I look at your furniture, layout, paint, and lighting, and rework what's there: keeping pieces that work, replacing what doesn't, and adding the layer of styling that makes a room feel finished. It suits a home with good bones that just needs a fresh eye, whether that's one room or the whole house.

Your home, finished.

A redesign is the fastest way to change how a home feels without touching a wall. Here's what's part of the process.

·

Furniture & layout

Deciding what to keep, what to rearrange, and where a room's traffic actually wants to flow.

·

Paint & color

A palette selected for your light and the rooms around it, not one color chosen in isolation.

·

New furnishings & textiles

Filling gaps with pieces that fit the scale of the room and the rest of what you own.

·

Lighting & styling

Lamps, art, and the final layer of styling that makes a room feel considered rather than staged.

Good to know

A renovation changes the structure of your home: walls, layout, plumbing. A redesign works with the structure you already have and changes furnishings, paint, lighting, and styling. No permits, no construction crew. If you're not sure which fits, the renovation page covers the other side.

Yes. Most redesigns start by looking at what you already own and deciding what to keep, what to rearrange, and what to replace, rather than starting from a blank room.

Either. Some clients want one room refreshed, like a living room or primary bedroom. Others want the whole home styled at once. We scope it to what you need on the discovery call.

A single room usually takes a few weeks from planning to install, mostly limited by furniture and fabric lead times. A whole-home redesign takes longer, since it covers more rooms and more selections.
