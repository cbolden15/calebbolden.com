This is how pricing works at Brittany Lyons Interiors. It is not a claim about how every Dallas designer charges. Good to know

There is no single flat rate. Every project starts with a complimentary discovery call, followed by a $350 in-home consultation when the project is a fit. Pricing is then built around the specific scope we define together.

Kitchens, primary bathrooms, full renovations, new construction, and select redesigns involve different materials, time, and coordination. Brittany Lyons Interiors prices the actual work rather than placing every project into the same package.
