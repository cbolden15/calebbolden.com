# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local and regional small-business owners and operators (10 to 200 employees): home services, property management, salons, clinics, law firms. They are doing diligence on AI help after a referral, a chamber talk, or a search. Most are not technical; they are skeptical of AI hype and short on time. Secondary audience: referral partners (CPAs, bookkeepers, MSPs, business bankers) checking whether Caleb is credible before sending a client.

## Product Purpose

The site converts local SMB owners into booked calls for the Process & AI Readiness Audit, and backs that with proof-first authority: shipped products, a visible method, and a live AI agent on the site itself. Success is a booked call, a workshop signup, or a completed self-assessment. Pricing is never published; packages show scope, deliverables, and timelines only.

## Brand Personality

Precise, hands-on, honest. The brand is the method: Caleb maps how work actually flows through a business (lean six sigma: value stream maps, kaizen workshops, annotation) and only then builds AI. The site should feel like a sharp operator's working wall, a process map mid-session: hairline grids, annotation marks, working-document warmth, builder confidence. First person voice: "I automate the work you shouldn't be doing."

## Anti-references

- AI-startup slop: purple gradients, glowing orbs, robot imagery, "unleash the power of AI" copy.
- Corporate consulting: stock handshake photos, blue-suit gravitas, deck-speak, invented statistics.
- Freelancer template: generic portfolio themes, three equal service cards, testimonial carousels.
- Crypto/tech-bro landing: dark mesh gradients, hype metrics, countdown urgency.

## Design Principles

1. The method is the identity. Mapping, annotation, and process motifs carry the brand; the visual system should look like the discipline it sells.
2. Practice what you preach. The site demonstrates the work: the chat assistant is a live product demo, the copy is plain, nothing is claimed that is not real.
3. Proof over promises. Shipped products and real artifacts only. No invented metrics, no fake logos, no testimonials until real ones exist.
4. Legible to a 55-year-old owner. Clarity beats cleverness everywhere; the path to "book a call" is never more than one glance away.
5. Motion explains, never decorates. Things draw, annotate, and connect the way a facilitator's marker does. Controlled, purposeful, reduced-motion safe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body contrast), visible focus states, full keyboard access to the chat and nav, prefers-reduced-motion honored on every animation, base font size never below 15px for body copy.
